# v4 — "a sweet box for Noah" (web digital gift): merged motion and style report

Source: 1024x576 screen recording, 610 frames at 30 fps (20.3 s). The capture is really **25 fps upconverted to 30 fps**: every 6th frame is a duplicate (9, 15, 21 … 99, 105 … 603, 609), so true temporal resolution is 40 ms and every timing below is ±1 frame (±33 ms). Easings were refit on de-duplicated 25 fps samples where the verifiers did so. Pixel sizes are at 1024 width. The page was probably captured from a ~1920 px browser, so multiply by ~1.875 for CSS px. TikTok watermark, logo bounce and end-card artifacts are ignored.

Merge rules applied: verifier corrections override analyst values; refuted events are dropped (v4B-09 landing spring, v4B-25 "f305 tap shift", v4A-E5 blur as an authored effect); missed events are added (v4A-M1, v4B-M1/M2, v4C-m01–m05); overlaps between segments (100–106 and 330–336) are deduped. One conflict was re-measured by the synthesizer: bow behaviour 104–125. Crop sheet: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-branch/56123255-aaa1-52c7-9cfa-8cb27117bf65/scratchpad/analysis/work/v4/synth_bow.jpg`. Mean |Δ| on the bow region vs f100 is 1.1–1.3 for 106–114, then 4.6 (118), 8.4 (121), 10.0 (124). Reconciled reading: 104–113 is sheen glints plus micro tail flutter (≤2 px, sub-threshold), and the real pull/tighten deformation runs 114–125, strong from 118.

## 1. Summary

The gift is a single-page "sweet box" ritual in five acts:

1. **Flower-curtain intro (f1–71).** A full-bleed watercolour flower collage holds for 3 frames. Every sprite then falls at once under gravity (per-sprite ballistic fall, g≈240 px/s²), drifting outward, tilting by quadrant and fading linearly. This reveals a static painted gingham tabletop with the wrapped gift. Clean at 2.33 s.
2. **Wrapped box (f71–136).** Idle life: a milkshake sways on an 800 ms trapezoid loop, hint rings pulse on the ribbon, and sheen glints run along the ribbon. Chrome arrives on timers: the "There's more here" pill and heart FAB hard-cut in at f91; the rose "continue" icon plus tooltip mount at f97. The user pulls the ribbon: the bow tightens (114–125), labels fade (122–125), the bow collapses (125–127), the ribbon whips off right with ease-in (130–133), and a crumpled pile appears (133–134).
3. **Open box (f136–203).** One ~280 ms easeInOutCubic motion flips the lid (perspective rotateX 0→180) while the box re-centres downward (136–143). Then a sticker pops with easeOutBack (+4%), a caption fades in, heart confetti rises and pennant flags fade in. The user taps treats: 6 bites total, 2 per treat, each with an instant sprite swap, a 1-frame heart charm, crumb sparkles and a box press-squash. The first bite of each treat pops a note bubble with progress copy.
4. **Letter spread (f204–336).** The 6th bite triggers automatic choreography: props fade (100 ms), the box slides to the left column (~280 ms easeInOutCubic), the "Dear Noah" letter pops (scale .93→1.012→1), hearts burst on its washi tape, three polaroids deal in at 150 ms stagger with rise and scale, a second deal of polaroids stacks on top, and a "play again" pill fades in. The heart FAB opens a dark plum "Your bouquet" voice-note drawer that slides in from the right (~500 ms easeOutQuart, spring ≈386/44, no dim).
5. **Extras and finale (f336–610).** The drawer scrolls (50 px, easeInOutQuad), snap-jumps to a YouTube song card plus a Memory map card, and the song plays from 1:00. Tapping the polaroid stack opens a lightbox: shared-element FLIP with spring overshoot ≈5% (k≈490, c≈33), a linear plum scrim (66–69%) and controls fading in afterwards. Tapping the gated rose CTA starts the finale: a ghost-flower veil, then a 130 ms pause, a spinning hero rose thrown on an ease-in arc, an impact flash (60% warm cream) with rays and a shockwave ring, a flower burst, and a flower wall rising at ~1400 px/s that floods the screen by f606 and keeps streaming.

Signature feel: painterly cut-out illustration, physical gestures (pull, bite, throw), quick ease-in exits paired with springy or ease-out entrances, small UI chrome at the edges, and flowers as both curtain and finale, so the experience is bookended.

## 5. Transitions

1. **Flower curtain → table** (f4–71, 2.23 s). Per-sprite gravity fall plus outward drift, quadrant tilt and linear fade. The table is static underneath, so the reveal reads top-down because the off-screen rank keeps the top covered. Implementation: absolutely positioned sprite layer over the mounted scene; `pointer-events:none` once done.
2. **Wrapped → open** (f114–148). Gesture-driven: untie, whip-off, a one-curve lid flip with re-centre, then the sticker stinger.
3. **Box stage → letter spread** (f204–256). Automatic on completion: props fade, box layout move (FLIP / Framer `layout`), letter pop, hearts, staggered polaroid deals, replay pill.
4. **Drawer in** (f310–325). Right slide plus fade, no dim.
5. **Drawer section change** (f373–403). Smooth scroll, then an instant snap.
6. **Lightbox open** (f522–541). Shared-element spring, linear scrim, delayed controls.
7. **Finale flower flood** (f570–610). Throw, impact, burst, rising wall. The wall is a flower curtain like the intro, so this likely loops into the opening state. Use it as the universal scene-change wipe.

## 7. Interaction flow

1. Open link (a user gesture also starts the music). The flower curtain falls automatically for 2.2 s; no input is needed.
2. Timers: chrome mounts at ~3.0 s (pill + FAB) and ~3.2 s (rose CTA + tooltip). Hint rings and glints invite the pull.
3. **Pull the ribbon** (drag the tail, ~50 px threshold; offer a tap fallback). About 1 s from release to full reveal.
4. **Tap treats.** Two bites each, 6 total. Notes reveal on the first bite with "N to go" progress.
5. **Completion** auto-advances to the letter spread (~1.5 s of choreography).
6. **Heart FAB** opens the extras drawer: voice note, bouquet, song embed, memory map. Content scrolls.
7. **Tap a polaroid** to open the lightbox gallery ("4 / 6", prev/next, close).
8. **Rose CTA** ("only tap when you've finished reading") starts the finale flower flood, which leads to the next scene or a loop.

Vietnamese copy suggestions: "kéo dải ruy băng" (pull the ribbon), "chạm vào một món để cắn thử" (tap a treat to take a bite), "còn 2 món nữa" (2 to go), "Chỉ chạm khi đã đọc xong để tiếp tục" (only tap when you've finished reading), "Còn nữa nè" (There's more here), "xem lại" (play again).

## 8. Pacing and audio sync

- Tempo is ~76 bpm (beat ≈ 789 ms ≈ 23.7 frames).
- The curtain starts on the first onset (f3–4), so the music and the intro share one start gesture.
- The milkshake's 800 ms sway loop equals one beat but drifts against it: it is a fixed CSS loop, not audio-locked. Even so, ~0.8 s idles feel on-tempo with 75 bpm music.
- All later events are user-paced, so near-beat alignments (sticker settle 146 ≈ beat 147; drawer 310–312 ≈ beat 312; CTA tap 570 ≈ beat 569) are coincidental or the creator timing taps by ear.
- Rhythm of a beat:
  - micro-holds of 67–133 ms between stages (127–129, 134–135, 571–574)
  - exits 67–133 ms with ease-in
  - entrances 100–280 ms with ease-out, back or spring
  - celebrations 300 ms – 1.2 s
- Finale timing after the tap:
  - 0 ms: UI out
  - 130 ms: launch
  - ~330 ms: impact
  - ~1.2 s: full cover

  Consider landing the impact on a downbeat.
- Suggested SFX: ribbon swish (130), box thup (142), crunch per bite, chime on a note pop, whoosh on the layout move and drawer, bloom whoosh on the finale.
- The song embed starts at 1:00, the chorus.
