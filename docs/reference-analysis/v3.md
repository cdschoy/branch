# v3: Canva "virtual gift" website (birthday, tap the cake, love letter): motion and style reverse-engineering

**Source:** `916e7fa9-v1c044g50000d78dnpnog65pigqgmsd0.mp4`, 576×1024 portrait at 30 fps, 789 frames. App content runs f1–669 (0.00–22.27 s). A hard cut at f670 starts the TikTok outro, which is out of scope.
**What it is:** a phone filming an iPad (landscape, Safari) that shows a multi-page Canva-built gift site. A TikTok serif caption sits over the top of the video and a TikTok watermark appears too. Neither is part of the app.
**Licensed character note:** the mascot is a licensed cartoon dog/beagle character. Its stickers, peeking head and line art are recorded here for **placement and behaviour only**. Do not reuse them. Replace them with original mascots.
**Method:** three segment analyses (A f1–272, B f266–522, C f516–669), each adversarially re-measured by a verifier. Measurement tools: per-glyph ink-step detection, per-region luma/chroma curves, sub-frame easing fits with a 1-frame exposure model, template tracking with rotation search, and step-1 zoom sheets. The synthesizer re-checked two conflicts:
1. **Text ink colour.** Pixel-luma analysis (below) resolved it.
2. **Cake tilt sign.** A zoom sheet (`work/v3/synth/cake_dir.jpg`) resolved it.

Time = (frame − 1) / 30 s.

## 1. Summary

The site is a **calm, flat, scrapbook-style "storybook"**. It has exactly four kinds of motion:

1. **Typewriter text.** Glyphs are pre-laid-out and centred, and each one snaps to full opacity in place on a **strictly linear, time-anchored schedule**. Spaces take one slot each. Line wraps add no pause, and centred lines never reflow. Measured rates are 127, 80.4, 88.9, 127 and 27.3 ms per character. They fit one rule: **msPerChar ≈ min(127 ms, ≈3.22 s ÷ characters)**. Short strings type at about 7.9 chars/s, and longer strings are compressed into about 3.2 s.
2. **Page crossfades.** These are opacity only, with no scale, slide or shared-element morph. Each lasts **≈200–270 ms with an ease-out curve** (easeOutCubic / easeOutQuad / css `ease`, which can't be told apart at 30 fps). The outgoing page leads the incoming one by about 0.5–1 frame. The cream and star background is a persistent layer under every page. Elements shared between consecutive pages (the pink card shell and the peeking mascot) stay pixel-stable, so a page change inside the card reads as a **content swap**.
3. **Raster "pops."** Stickers and photos appear at full opacity in about one frame, 100–270 ms after a page starts. Photo slots show flat pink placeholder discs until the image arrives. This is almost certainly image-decode latency, but it reads as a cute snap.
4. **One idle wobble.** The tappable cake sways, dips and tilts irregularly (x ±8 px, y up to +12 px, rotate −1.5° to +10°, easeInOutSine segments of 0.4–0.6 s). It is the **only affordance** for "tap me." Nothing else idles: stars, stickers, buttons and photos are static.

There are **no press states** on any button, and taps are never on camera. The 1-frame "brighten" at f384 and f506 is the outgoing page's first faded frame painted before the incoming page arrives. It is not a press flash. The flow has five steps: birthday headline → "wanna see it?" yes/no → "tap on the cake" card → gift reveal (plush toy) plus a "my wishes" pill → love-letter collage card with circular couple photos. Every step is a browser-history navigation.

### Typewriter rate rule (derived; confidence 0.65)
| Block | Slots n | Measured ms/slot | n × rate | min(127, 3220/n) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | 22 | 127.0 | 2794 ms | 127 ✓ |
| Question | 40 | 80.4 | 3216 ms | 80.5 ✓ |
| Prompt | 34 | 88.9 | 3023 ms | 94.7 (✗ 6%; fits exactly if 2 invisible trailing slots: 3220/36 = 89.4) |
| Caption | 18 | 127.3 | 2291 ms | 127 ✓ |
| Letter | 118 | 27.3 | 3221 ms | 27.3 ✓ |

The question and the letter both total **3.22 s within 0.2%**, so the reference behaves like a typewriter with a **max speed of about 127 ms/char and a total-duration cap of about 3.2 s**.

**First-glyph delay** after the page change: 150–180 ms (question), ≈117 ms (prompt), 200 ms (caption), 67 ms (letter). That is roughly 1.5–2.5 slots, so use `startDelay = clamp(1.6 × msPerChar, 60, 200) ms`.

## 4. Motion primitives (reusable, distilled)

1. **`typewriter`**. Pre-laid-out per-grapheme opacity snap on a time-anchored linear schedule.
   - Split NFC text with `new Intl.Segmenter('vi', {granularity:'grapheme'})` so Vietnamese stacked diacritics (ệ, ữ, ở, ặ) stay attached to their base letter. Keep spaces as spans (`white-space: pre-wrap` keeps double spaces).
   - Render the whole paragraph first (`text-align:center`), with every span at `opacity:0`.
   - Span i gets `animation: glyphIn 16ms linear forwards; animation-delay: calc(var(--start) + var(--i) * var(--ms))`, with `@keyframes glyphIn { to { opacity:1 } }`.
   - `--ms = min(127ms, 3220ms / n)`. Delays come from t0 (not chained timers), so jank catches up.
   - No caret, no translate, no blur.
   - Add tap-to-complete and `prefers-reduced-motion` (show everything at once).
   - Framer Motion equivalent: parent `staggerChildren: ms/1000, delayChildren: start/1000`; child `{opacity:[0,1]}`, `transition: {duration: 0.02, ease: 'linear'}`.
2. **`page-crossfade`**. Opacity-only crossfade of absolutely stacked pages.
   - Token: **240 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)`** (easeOutCubic). Measured range is 140–270 ms across all dissolves.
   - The outgoing page may start 1 frame (≈17–33 ms) earlier, but simultaneous start is a faithful simplification.
   - Pixels shared by both pages never dip (linear "plus-lighter" dissolve). Use the **View Transitions API** default crossfade (`document.startViewTransition`, root `mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter`), or keep shared chrome (background, card shell, mascot) outside the fading layers.
   - Framer: `<AnimatePresence mode="sync">` with `position:absolute; inset:0` pages, `initial {opacity:0}`, `animate {opacity:1}`, `exit {opacity:0}`.
   - Push a history entry per page on the same tick.
3. **`content-swap-in-shell`**. A persistent card shell and its ornaments (peeking mascot) stay put. Only the inner content (key = step) crossfades out in about 200 ms (ease/linear) while the next CTA fades in over the same window (167 ms easeOutCubic).
4. **`sticker-pop`**. Raster illustration, photo or sticker appears in ≤1 frame, **100–270 ms after the page change starts**.
   - Intentional version: `decode()` first, then `opacity 0→1` over 0–33 ms at delay 120–150 ms (cartoon props) or about 260 ms (the "gift" reveal).
   - Multiple photos: stagger in reading order by about 33 ms (the reference); 80–120 ms reads better.
   - No scale or bounce in the reference.
5. **`placeholder-disc → photo`**. Each circular photo frame is first a flat disc about 8% darker than the card, inside its ring. The image replaces it with a near-step swap. Polished option: 180–220 ms fade.
6. **`idle-wobble` (tap affordance)**. Irregular keyframe loop on the tap target only.
   - `animate={{ x:[0,-2,0,8,-8,0], y:[0,3,12,0,10,0], rotate:[2,3,0,-1,10,0] }}`
   - `transition={{ duration:3.8, times:[0,.12,.45,.62,.85,1], ease:'easeInOut' /* sine-like */, repeat:Infinity }}`
   - `style={{ transformOrigin:'50% 90%' }}`
   - Scale x/y amplitudes to the asset: about ±4% of its width horizontally, +6% vertically, with a +10° clockwise tilt peak.
   - Everything else on screen stays still, which is why it reads as "tap me."
7. **`static-hold`**. No ambient loops (no twinkle, bob or parallax). The calm comes from stillness plus the reading pauses the user controls.
8. **`no-press-state` (reference) / `soft-press` (recommended)**. The reference has none. If you add one, use `whileTap={{ scale: 0.97 }}` for 60–80 ms and start the crossfade on pointerup. Set `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`.

**Ink colour resolution.** The segment C analyst read the letter text as "dark wine #4D1D1D"; verifier B read it as near-black with chroma bleed. The synthesizer measured luma at f300, f470 and f640:
- The darkest 10% of text pixels on pink are about [42,0,0] (Y ≈ 3–13), as dark as the black headline on cream ([6,5,0]).
- Their R−G ≈ 42–50 equals what full 4:2:0 chroma bleed from the card (Cr ≈ 156) adds to a black core (predicted [47,0,0]).
- A true #4D1D1D or #5A1F22 could not film that dark (its own luma is 40–49).

So the ink is **near-black**. Its exact hue is unmeasurable on pink. A very dark warm black that harmonises with the pink is a safe design choice.

## 7. Interaction flow

1. **Arrive (page 1):** the headline auto-types. The single CTA "next :3" is visible and tappable from load (not gated by typing). The user waited about 1.3 s after typing ended.
2. **Tap "next :3"** → no press state → history push (Safari back enables at f129–130) → dissolve 1 (≈250 ms) → the question types at 80 ms/char.
3. **Binary gate:** "yes!!" and "no thanks." are styled equally on either side of the mascot. The "no thanks." outcome was not captured. The user tapped "yes!!" about 1.3 s after typing ended.
4. **Tap "yes!!"** → dissolve 2 → pink card with peeking mascot and wobbling cake. The typed **diegetic instruction** "tap on the cake to open your gift!" names the hit target (the illustration itself, no button chrome).
5. **Tap the cake** (about 0.8 s after typing ended) → content swap inside the persistent card → gift photo pops (+267 ms), caption types at 127 ms/char. The next CTA "my wishes" appears right away, docked on the card edge.
6. **Tap "my wishes"** (about 1.6 s after typing) → dissolve 3 → love-letter collage. The long letter types fast (27 ms/char, ≈3.2 s total). Photos and stickers fill their slots.
7. **End:** the letter rests. No completion cue, replay or close control was captured.

Recommendations for the Vietnamese gift-card and photobooth apps:
- Keep the tap-the-object gate (gift box, envelope, phong bì, or the booth shutter) with `idle-wobble` as the only motion.
- Preload and decode the next step's images.
- Add soft press feedback, tap-to-skip typing and reduced-motion support.
- Add a gentle completion cue 600–1000 ms after the last glyph (heart pop or "chạm để tiếp tục" hint).

## 8. Pacing and audio sync

- **Soundtrack:** TikTok-added music, 92.3 BPM (beats every ≈650 ms at f7, 27, 46 … 654, 672). There are **no UI sounds**, and nothing is beat-locked. All page changes are user taps. Their offsets from beats (+4…+5 frames) and the f520 photo pop (1 frame after beat 519, decode-driven) are coincidences.
- **Typewriter cadence** (127 / 80 / 89 / 27 ms) is unrelated to the beat. There are 62+ glyph reveals but only ≈34 onsets in the first 9 s, so there is no per-key click.
- **Rhythm of the experience:** each page = crossfade (≈0.25 s) + typing (2.2–3.2 s) + reader-controlled hold (0.8–1.6 s). That is about 4–5 s per beat of the story, ≈22 s end to end.
- **If adding sound:** a soft chime or whoosh at each page crossfade, a "pop" on the gift reveal, and optionally a quiet tick per glyph only when ≥80 ms/char. At 27 ms/char ticks would be noise; use one chime at the letter start and a sparkle on completion. Quantising the reveal pop to the next beat is optional; the reference does not do it.

## 10. Open questions and low-confidence items

- **Pops vs designed stagger:** the raster pops (f272, 392, 509, 520–522) have variable delays (100–533 ms), and there is a 200 ms freeze, so they are probably decode latency rather than authored motion. The recommended rebuild makes them deliberate.
- **Typewriter rule:** `min(127, 3220/n)` fits 4 of 5 blocks exactly. The prompt is 6% off unless it has 2 invisible trailing slots. Confidence 0.65.
- **Per-glyph reveal:** an instant swap (≤17 ms) is the best estimate. A 30–60 ms fade would look slightly softer than the reference.
- **Crossfade compositing:** the evidence for a linear "plus-lighter" dissolve (shared pixels stable) comes from dissolves 3–4 only. Dissolve 1's stars are persistent either way.
- **Dissolve curve family:** easeOutCubic vs easeOutQuad vs css `ease` can't be separated at 30 fps. Durations range from 140 to 270 ms per side.
- **Cake wobble:** this is probably a looping animated (GIF) sticker. The loop period is not confirmed (only 3.7 s observed). Keyframe amplitudes come from rotation-search tracking (conf 0.75).
- **Not captured:** the "no thanks." branch, the page-1 entrance (the video starts with the headline empty and the sticker already visible), any end-of-letter navigation, and the left part of the iPad screen (cropped; page-2 line art bleeds off it).
- **Fonts:** unidentified Canva handwriting. The distinctive figure-8 "g" and upsilon "u" need a custom font or glyph substitution to match exactly. Mali or Patrick Hand are the practical Vietnamese-safe choices.
- **Colours:** card pink (#F6B8B4–#FDC6C0), outline hue and ink hue on pink are estimates. A clean screenshot would settle them.
- **Card radius:** the prompt card (≈16–22 filmed px) and the letter card (≈12 px) radii differ. They may be one CSS value seen through perspective, or two settings.
- **Licensed character:** placement and behaviour only. Replace all mascot art.
