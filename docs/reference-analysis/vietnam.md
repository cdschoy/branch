# Vietnamese localization and cultural adaptation

Products: **(A) gift card** (the recipient opens a link: envelope/box/booth reveal, letter, photos, songs, memories) and **(B) self-photobooth** (camera, countdown, flash, strips, frames/stickers, save/share).

Companion files:
- `design/vi.json`: 346 UI strings in vi + en, with English dotted keys, pronoun sets and tone particles. It is generated by `design/tools/build_vi.py`, which checks NFC, key parity and placeholder parity. Always edit the script and re-run it; never hand-edit the JSON.
- `design/tools/amlich.py`: Hồ Ngọc Đức lunar-calendar algorithm at UTC+7, used for every lunar date below.
- Aligns with `design/visual.md` §3 (approved fonts) and `design/motion-tokens.json` (primitive names).

Evidence is cited as `vN fA-B` (30 fps frames from the verified reports). **IP rule:** we reproduce motion feel and visual language only. All art is original illustration. We do not use the reference flowers, booth, box art, brand names, or the v3 licensed mascot.

## 0. Decisions at a glance

1. **The sender chooses xưng hô** (anh–em, em–anh, tớ–cậu, mình–bạn, none) and a **tone** (Bắc "nhé" / Nam "nha"). Gift copy is written in the sender's voice through `{self} {you} {we} {nha}` placeholders. App chrome always speaks to the user as "bạn".
2. **Default skins per season**, not one global look: lì xì + mai/đào for Tết, hoa sữa / cúc họa mi for 20/10 and November, a mooncake box + lanterns for Trung Thu, peony/rose for 14/2 (closest to the reference palette, v5 f100-225).
3. **Open-in-browser is a first-class flow.** Most links will be opened inside Zalo or Messenger. Camera, download and audio must each degrade gracefully (§5.1).
4. **Fonts:** only the 74/74 verified list. Default stack is Be Vietnam Pro (UI), Mali (hand), Dancing Script (script), Playfair Display (serif), Paytone One (display). The typewriter splits text by grapheme cluster, never by code unit.
5. **Payments:** VietQR bank transfer + MoMo + ZaloPay + VNPay, priced in whole VND. The server-side IPN/webhook is the only source of truth.
6. **Data:** photos and voice are personal data. Consent is opt-in and separate per purpose. Booth processing is on-device by default. There is no face or voice recognition. Gifts auto-expire. Comply with Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP **and** the Personal Data Protection Law (in force 1/1/2026, see §5.5).
7. **Hosting:** Singapore origin + a CDN with Hà Nội/TP.HCM edges for static assets and media. Get a legal check on whether a Vietnamese entity must also keep data in Vietnam.

### Day-count culture ("yêu nhau được bao nhiêu ngày")
- Store `startDate` as a calendar date in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` (UTC+7, no DST). Count whole calendar days. Never divide timestamps by 86 400 000 across time zones.
- Add the setting `create.memories.countFirstDay`, **default ON**: the start day is day 1. Most VN couple day-counter apps count this way, and users expect today's number to be one higher than the plain difference.
- Milestones to surface: 100, 200, 300, 365, 500, 520 ("520" sounds like "I love you" in Chinese and is known in VN youth culture; optional), 1000, 1314 ("một đời một kiếp" pun, optional), plus yearly and monthly anniversaries. Use `gift.days.untilMilestone` for a countdown.
- Show `{years} năm {months} tháng {days} ngày` as a secondary line.
- **Scheduled opening:** let senders lock a gift until a moment such as "00:00 ngày 14/2" or the Giao thừa minute (`gift.lock.*`). Always display the time as "Giờ Việt Nam (GMT+7)".

### 2.2 Photobooth culture (product B)
- The VN mall norm (Vincom/AEON-style malls, near universities) is the **Korean-style 4-cut strip**: themed frames that change monthly, a prop basket (headbands, glasses), 8-10 s timers, the subject choosing the best 4 of 6-8 shots, and 2 prints to share with a friend. Reproduce the *ritual* in B:
  1. Layout pick: `booth.layout.*`, with `strip4` the default.
  2. "Thả xu để chụp ✦": v1's coin is the start ritual, as a free virtual coin.
  3. Pose prompts between shots: `booth.pose.*`, e.g. "Bắn tim nào" (finger heart).
  4. Optionally take 6 shots and pick 4.
  5. "Đang in…" or "Đang rửa ảnh…".
  6. A **2-up export** (two strips side by side on one image) for "one for you, one for me".
- Frames rotate seasonally (§1). Sticker words come from youth speech: "thương", "iu", "mãi bên nhau", "cười lên", "xinh xỉu", "chill", "hihi", "nhớ nè". Keep teencode light. Avoid "j" for "gì" and "ko" in UI chrome; stickers may use them.
- Filter names are original and evocative, not brand names: Sữa, Phim cũ, Đen trắng, Hồng đào, Sương sớm (`booth.filter.*`).

### 2.4 Avoid list (kitsch, political, religious, superstition)
- **National symbols:** no red field + yellow five-pointed star combination, no hammer and sickle, no portraits of leaders, no state emblems. Use red + gold freely in other forms (lacquer, mai), but never as a flag pattern.
- **Maps** (the Memory map, v1/v4 drawer): any map must show Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa as part of Vietnam, label the sea "Biển Đông", and never show the nine/ten-dash line. Use a map provider in the `vi` locale and test the viewport at country zoom, or use an original stylised local map with no national borders at all. This is a legal and PR risk in VN.
- **Religion:** no Buddha figures, altars (bàn thờ), incense, pagoda interiors, Christian sacred imagery, or deities (Thần Tài, Ông Táo) as decoration or jokes. The lotus is fine as a flower, but never with Buddha or altar context, and never on "sexy" or flirty frames. Christmas stays secular (snow, pine, lights). Avoid Vu Lan (15/7 âm) and "tháng cô hồn" themes completely. Thất Tịch also falls in lunar month 7, so keep its theme on the legend (bridge, rain, stars), with no ghosts and no offerings.
- **Funeral cues (critical for the photobooth):**
  - Never combine a single centred black-and-white portrait with a black frame or a black ribbon. That reads as a **di ảnh** (memorial portrait). B&W 4-cut strips are fine, so keep a light frame on single B&W shots.
  - Avoid large white chrysanthemum arrangements, white headbands (khăn tang), and all-white bouquets with black ribbons.
- **Money:** never reproduce Vietnamese banknotes (đồng). Imitating currency is regulated. Lì xì shows only the envelope.
- **Hán tự:** avoid Chinese characters (福, 囍) as decoration. Use Vietnamese quốc ngữ calligraphy (thư pháp chữ Việt: "An", "Thương", "Phúc") in an original hand.
- **Kitsch / postcard tropes:** avoid the "rice field + nón lá + buffalo" tourist postcard and conical-hat overload. One nón lá prop sticker is fine. Áo dài appears as an elegant silhouette (hem flutter), never sexualised. No "Saigon girl" or "Hanoi girl" stereotypes.
- **Superstition, kept light:** 4 is weakly unlucky (tứ ≈ tử) in Sino-Vietnamese tradition, but 4-cut strips are a mainstream norm, so keep them. Avoid gifting-copy jokes about breaking up near Tết.
- **The v3 mascot** and any licensed characters: none. The sticker sheet is all original.

## 3. Microcopy deck (`vi.json`)

### 3.1 Register
- **App voice** (`create.*`, `booth.*`, `pay.*`, `privacy.*`, `inapp.*`, `common.*`, `a11y.*`):
  - Warm, friendly and polite. The app calls the user **"bạn"**, and particles like "nhé" stay gentle.
  - Short imperative + particle ("Kiểm tra lại liên kết nhé.").
  - No "quý khách" (too formal) and no teencode in chrome.
  - Legal and consent strings drop the particles and use "Tôi đồng ý…" (clear, unambiguous consent wording).
- **Sender voice** (`gift.*` except `gift.error.*`, plus the prefilled `create.share.message` and `create.message.suggest*`):
  - Uses the pronoun placeholders `{self} {Self} {you} {You} {we} {We}` and the tone particle `{nha}` → "nhé" (Bắc) / "nha" (Nam).
  - Playful particles that work in both regions ("nè", "rồi đó") are written inline.
- **Pronoun sets** (`pronouns` in `vi.json`):

  | id | self / you / we | Example (`gift.landing.from`) |
  |---|---|---|
  | `anh-em` | anh / em / mình | "Anh có một món quà nhỏ cho em nè" |
  | `em-anh` | em / anh / mình | "Em có một món quà nhỏ cho anh nè" |
  | `to-cau` | tớ / cậu / tụi mình (Bắc: bọn mình) | "Tớ có một món quà nhỏ cho cậu nè" |
  | `minh-ban` | mình / bạn / chúng mình | "Mình có một món quà nhỏ cho bạn nè" |
  | `none` | mình / {recipientName} / chúng mình | "Mình có một món quà nhỏ cho Bống nè" |

  Resolution order: tone `weOverride` → pronoun set → `{nha}` → names. Use the capitalised variants only at sentence start. **Never** use CSS `text-transform: capitalize` on Vietnamese; it can hit a name or a lowercase pronoun.
- **Names:** Vietnamese people are addressed by their *given* name (the last syllable: "Nguyễn Thị **Lan**"), so the creator asks for a **tên gọi / biệt danh** (`create.names.nickname`) and uses it in `{recipientName}`. Do not auto-split full names.
- **Counts:** Vietnamese has no plural inflection ("1 ảnh", "5 ảnh"). The English side may be wrapped in an ICU `plural` at integration time.
- **Typography in copy:**
  - Use "…" (one character), the "×" sign in 2×2, and "›" in settings paths.
  - Use curly “ ” quotes in Vietnamese.
  - Put a no-break space between number and unit when rendering (`15 giây`, `29.000 ₫`).

## 4. Typography rules for Vietnamese

### 4.1 Font eligibility (from `metrics/fonts_vietnamese.txt`, glyph-verified 74/74)
- **Approved:** Be Vietnam Pro, Mali, Patrick Hand, Itim, Pangolin, Dancing Script, Charm, Pacifico, Great Vibes, Playfair Display, Paytone One, Dela Gothic One, Cherry Bomb One, Grandstander, Borel, Shantell Sans, Sedgwick Ave, Coiny, Anton, Playwrite VN.
  - Playwrite VN is served unsplit, so its CSS subset flag reads 0, but its coverage is full. It is a Vietnamese school-handwriting face, which makes it ideal for a "handwritten letter".
- **Banned** (glyph check failed, or no VN subset): Caveat, Caveat Brush, Sacramento, Parisienne, Fredoka, Bebas Neue, Lilita One, Gochi Hand, Special Elite, Kalam, Archivo Black, Courier Prime, Cutive Mono, Bodoni Moda, DM Serif Display, Young Serif, Chewy, Sniglet, Delius, Short Stack, Bagel Fat One, Bowlby One, Shrikhand, Gloock, Darumadrop One. The v2a report suggests Archivo Black; that is overruled.
- **Subset-flag only, not glyph-checked:** Montserrat, Nunito, Quicksand, Lexend, Lora, Inter, Space Mono, etc. Use them only after running the same fontTools 74-glyph check.
- **Role map** (same as visual.md §3.2):

  | Role | Font | Used for |
  |---|---|---|
  | UI | Be Vietnam Pro | Chrome and controls |
  | Hand | Mali | Letters and notes |
  | Script | Dancing Script | Prompts ("Chạm để mở", as in v5 "Tap to open") |
  | Serif | Playfair Display | Headings |
  | Display | Paytone One | Countdown numerals and strip headers |

- For a **typewriter/receipt "Đang in…" look**, the reference mono faces fail. Use Be Vietnam Pro with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` and letter-spacing 0.02em instead.

### 4.2 Text normalisation
- Run `s.normalize('NFC')` on **every** user input (names, letter, captions, secret answers) at input time and again server-side.
  - macOS/iOS copy-paste and some files arrive as NFD ("e" + U+0302 + U+0301).
  - NFD breaks length limits and matching, and can render detached marks in some fonts.
- **Secret-answer matching:** NFC → `toLocaleLowerCase('vi')` → trim/collapse spaces → compare. Also accept a "loose" match: strip diacritics with NFD + remove `\p{M}`, map đ→d, and accept it only when the user typed no diacritics at all (common on phones with no-accent keyboards).
- **Old vs new tone placement** (hoà/hòa, thuỷ/thủy): map both to one canonical form before comparing. Never rewrite the user's displayed text.
- **Legacy encodings:** if pasted text contains TCVN3/VNI mojibake patterns (e.g. "Cña", "®"), show "Văn bản có thể bị lỗi phông chữ" and offer a converter later. Do not guess-convert silently.
- **Length limits** count grapheme clusters (`Intl.Segmenter`), not `.length`. `.length` counts "ệ" as 1 in NFC but 3 in NFD.
- **Case:** use `toLocaleUpperCase('vi')` / `toLocaleLowerCase('vi')`. Use `localeCompare(b, 'vi')` / `Intl.Collator('vi')` for sorting (Vietnamese order: a ă â b c d đ e ê …).

### 4.3 Typewriter (v3 rule) with grapheme segmentation
```js
const seg = new Intl.Segmenter('vi', { granularity: 'grapheme' });
export function typewriterSpans(el, text) {
  const t = text.normalize('NFC');
  const graphemes = [...seg.segment(t)].map(s => s.segment);   // "ệ","ữ","👩‍❤️‍👨" stay whole
  el.textContent = '';
  const frag = document.createDocumentFragment();
  for (const g of graphemes) {
    const s = document.createElement('span');
    s.textContent = g; s.style.opacity = '0';                    // pre-laid-out, never reflows (v3 §1.1)
    frag.append(s);
  }
  el.append(frag);
  const msPer = Math.min(127, 3220 / graphemes.length);          // v3 f11-604 rate rule
  return { spans: [...el.children], msPer };
}
```
- Segment **after** fonts have loaded: `await document.fonts.load('400 20px Mali', 'ắệữ')`. The sample text must contain Vietnamese letters, because Google Fonts splits the Vietnamese `unicode-range` into its own file, which only downloads when such a character is requested. Without it, the first frames render tone marks in the fallback font.
- If the browser lacks `Intl.Segmenter` (old Android WebView < Chrome 87 / Firefox < 125), fall back to the regex `/\P{M}\p{M}*/gu`. Under NFC, every Vietnamese letter is one code point anyway; the regex protects NFD leftovers and emoji modifiers.
- Wrap each **word** in a `white-space: nowrap` span that holds its letter spans, so a line never breaks inside a syllable.
- The same segmentation applies to the v2a **per-glyph mosaic stagger** and to v2b **word-by-word captions** (use `granularity: 'word'`).

### 4.4 Metrics and layout
- **Line-height:** UI/serif ≥ 1.5. Handwriting 1.6-1.65. Script ≥ 1.7 (Great Vibes 1.75). ALL CAPS ≥ 1.3. Stacked marks (ẫ, ỗ, Ặ, Ễ) collide with the line above below these values.
- Leave **top padding of ≥ 0.15em** on single-line pills, stamps and countdown bubbles, and on any `overflow: hidden` container (the v1 print slot), so the marks on capitals are not clipped. Test with "ỄẶỖ Ừ g".
- **Tracking:** caps ≤ 0.15em. Never letter-space script fonts.
- **Faux styles:**
  - Set `font-synthesis: none` globally for script and display faces. Pacifico, Great Vibes, Dancing Script and Paytone One have no italic, and synthetic oblique skews the stacked marks off their base letters.
  - Never apply `font-style: italic` or `<em>` to script text. Emphasise with colour or weight, or switch to Playfair Display Italic (a real italic).
- **Minimum size:** 14 px for body and 12 px for captions. At 11 px and below, dot-below marks (ạ, ệ, ợ) merge into the baseline on low-DPI Android.
- **Line breaking:**
  - Vietnamese breaks at spaces. Use `hyphens: manual` and never auto-hyphenate.
  - Use `text-wrap: balance` for headings and `text-wrap: pretty` for letters.
  - Keep a pronoun or particle bound to its neighbour with a no-break space where it matters ("cho em", "nhé" never alone on a line).
- **Canvas text on photostrips:**
  - `await document.fonts.load(...)` with Vietnamese sample text before `fillText`.
  - Set `ctx.textRendering = 'optimizeLegibility'` where supported.
  - Measure with `measureText` per line. Do not use `letterSpacing` on scripts.
- **Text extrusion** (v2a/v2b 3D words): layered `text-shadow` only. Do not stroke tone marks separately.
- **Loading:**
  - Use Google Fonts CSS2 with `display=swap`.
  - Preload Be Vietnam Pro 500 + Mali 400 + the theme's script face (the Vietnamese-range WOFF2 files).
  - Self-host them for the first-load page (one fewer origin; see §5.6).

## 5. Market and technical constraints

### 5.1 In-app browsers (Zalo, Messenger/Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
Most gift links will be opened from **Zalo** (the dominant messenger in VN) and **Messenger**. Treat the in-app webview as the *primary* environment.
- **Detection** (UA heuristics; also combine them with capability checks):

  | App | UA tokens |
  |---|---|
  | Zalo | `Zalo` |
  | Facebook / Messenger | `FBAN`, `FBAV`, `FB_IAB`, `Messenger` |
  | Instagram | `Instagram` |
  | TikTok | `musical_ly`, `BytedanceWebview`, `TikTok` |

- **Camera (`getUserMedia`):**
  - Unreliable or unavailable in these webviews.
    - Android WebView only works if the host app implements `onPermissionRequest`.
    - iOS WKWebView (iOS 14.3+) works only if the host app opts in.
  - **Never assume it works:**
    - Call it inside a user gesture.
    - Catch `NotAllowedError`, `NotFoundError`, `NotReadableError`, `OverconstrainedError`, `SecurityError`, and the case where `navigator.mediaDevices` is undefined.
    - Map each one to a `booth.error.*` string.
  - Fallback ladder for B:
    1. `getUserMedia` (best: live preview, countdown, flash).
    2. If it fails in an in-app browser, show the `inapp.banner.*` sheet with platform steps. On **Android**, offer an `intent://` URL to Chrome: `intent://{host}{path}#Intent;scheme=https;package=com.android.chrome;end`. On **iOS** there is no programmatic escape, so show "⋯ → open in Safari" + `inapp.copyForSafari`.
    3. **`<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="user">`** (`booth.fallback.nativeCamera`). This opens the native camera in nearly every webview. Collect 4 shots one at a time (`booth.fallback.progress`), then compose the same strip. Keep the countdown/flash animation as a post-capture "develop" moment.
    4. "Chọn ảnh có sẵn" (gallery picker).
- **Downloads:** `<a download>` with blob URLs usually fails in the Zalo/FB webviews. Render the final strip as a real `<img src="blob:…">` and show `booth.save.longPress` ("Nhấn giữ ảnh để lưu vào máy"). This works in most webviews.
- **Audio:** autoplay is blocked everywhere. The first "Chạm để mở" tap is the unlock gesture (v4 §7.1: music starts on the opening gesture). Start a silent `AudioContext` in that handler. Webviews may pause media when the app backgrounds, so resume on `visibilitychange`.
- **Link previews:**
  - Zalo and Messenger render Open Graph tags: `og:title`, `og:description` (in Vietnamese), and `og:image` 1200×630.
  - The preview must **not** reveal the gift contents or the recipient's photos. Use a generic themed envelope image + "{senderName} gửi bạn một món quà".
  - Zalo caches previews aggressively. Version the share URL (`?v=`) when the OG image changes.
- **Viewport:**
  - Webviews add top/bottom chrome. Use `100dvh` with a `100vh` fallback, plus `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
  - Do not put critical CTAs in the bottom 56 px, where Zalo's toolbar sits on some Android builds.
- Ship the **open-in-browser sheet as optional** for product A (most of A works in a webview) and **mandatory before camera** for product B.

### 5.2 Low-end Android share/save
- **Target:** 2-3 GB RAM Android devices with Chrome/WebView 90+, which are common in VN (budget Samsung A, Oppo A, Xiaomi Redmi, Vivo Y).
- **Capture:** request `{ width: { ideal: 1280 }, height: { ideal: 720 }, facingMode: 'user' }`, never 4K. Downscale each shot to ≤ 1080 px on its long edge via `createImageBitmap(video, { resizeWidth })`.
- **Compose the strip** at ≤ 1200 × 3600 px with `canvas.toBlob('image/jpeg', 0.9)`. Use WebP only if `toBlob` supports it; feature-test, because Safari < 14 lacks WebP encoding. Release bitmaps with `.close()` and revoke object URLs.
- **Filters:**
  - Implement as a small LUT/pixel loop in a Worker (OffscreenCanvas where available), or in WebGL.
  - `ctx.filter` is not universal on iOS Safari, so don't rely on it.
  - On `booth.error.lowMemory`, drop to 720 px.
- **Share:** use `navigator.canShare({ files: [file] })` → `navigator.share({ files, title, text })`. This works on Chrome Android and iOS Safari 15+, but not in most webviews. Fall back to save + `booth.share.unsupported`.
- **Link share for A:**
  - `navigator.share({ url })`.
  - A Zalo share button (Zalo's official share plugin) and a Messenger deep link on mobile.
  - Always "Sao chép liên kết", plus a QR code (`create.share.qr`). QR is common for in-person gifting.
- **Weight budget:**
  - First paint ≤ 300 KB JS + CSS.
  - Gift intro media ≤ 1.5 MB before the first interaction. Progressively load later acts.
  - Sprite sheets as WebP/AVIF with JPEG fallbacks.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and add an in-app "Giảm hiệu ứng" toggle (`a11y.reducedMotion`). Screen-flash photosensitivity uses the `booth.flashWarning` opt-out.

### 5.3 Payments for premium themes (MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay, VietQR)
- **Prices** in whole VND:
  - Format with `new Intl.NumberFormat('vi-VN', { style: 'currency', currency: 'VND' })`, which gives "29.000 ₫". Many local sites write "29.000đ", so pick one and be consistent.
  - Suggested tiers: 0 ₫ (base themes), 19.000-29.000 ₫ (one premium theme), 49.000-79.000 ₫ (seasonal bundle: Tết / Trung Thu / 20/10).
  - Students are price-sensitive. Seasonal bundles near 14/2, 8/3, 20/10 and Tết convert best.
- **Methods, in suggested order:**
  1. **VietQR / NAPAS 24/7 bank transfer.** Near-universal, the lowest fees, and works from any banking app. Generate a dynamic QR with amount + a unique memo (`pay.transferNote`). Confirm through a bank-transaction webhook service or your bank's API.
  2. **MoMo** (largest e-wallet). Payment gateway with an app deeplink + QR.
  3. **ZaloPay.** A natural fit since links travel via Zalo; gateway with deeplink/QR.
  4. **VNPay.** Gateway covering bank apps (VNPAY-QR) and domestic cards.
  5. An international card via a global processor for overseas Vietnamese.
- **Flow rules:**
  - The server creates the order, then the client shows the QR or deeplink, then **the IPN/webhook marks it paid** (signature verified), then the client polls or uses SSE until confirmed (`pay.pending`).
  - Never trust the redirect `returnUrl` alone.
  - Make the IPN handler idempotent.
  - Order expiry is 10-15 min (`pay.expiresIn`).
- **Webview gotchas:** `momo://` / `zalopay://` deeplinks may be blocked inside other apps' webviews. Always show the **QR alongside the "Mở ứng dụng" button**. On desktop, show the QR only. After an app switch, re-poll on `visibilitychange`.
- **Business prerequisites:** the gateways (MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay) require a registered business (giấy phép kinh doanh) and merchant onboarding. Issue e-invoices (hóa đơn điện tử) where required by Vietnamese tax rules (verify with an accountant). If a native app is ever built, Apple/Google in-app purchase rules apply to digital themes.
- **Unlock scope:** unlock per gift, or per account, for "restore purchases" (`pay.restore`). Store the receipt by order ID, never card data.

### 5.4 Music sources and embedding
| Source | Embed in page? | Notes |

### 5.5 Personal data protection (Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP and the 2025 PDP Law)
> Not legal advice. The **Law on Personal Data Protection (Law 91/2025/QH15)** took effect on **1/1/2026** and supersedes Decree 13 for most matters, with its own implementing decree. Have Vietnamese counsel confirm the current obligations, thresholds and any small-business/startup exemptions. The design below is meant to satisfy the stricter reading of both.

- **What we process:**
  - Basic personal data: names, nicknames, photos showing faces, letters, relationship dates, place names in the memory map, voice recordings, IP/device data, payment references.
  - **Sensitive data to avoid creating:** biometric data (face or voice used for identification), sex life / orientation inferences, precise location.
  - Rules that follow:
    - **No face recognition, face-ID clustering, or voice-print features.** If AR stickers need face landmarks, run them **on-device only** and never transmit or store the landmarks. Say so (`privacy.noFaceId`).
    - The memory map stores user-typed place names, not GPS.
- **Consent (opt-in, specific, per purpose, provable):**
  - Separate, **unticked** checkboxes for photos (`privacy.consent.photos`), voice (`privacy.consent.voice`) and marketing (`privacy.consent.marketing`, optional). Silence or a pre-ticked box is not consent.
  - Show the notice (`privacy.notice`) **before** processing: purposes, data types, retention, storage location, rights, and the controller's contact.
  - Log consent with a timestamp, the policy version, and the gift/account ID.
  - **Third parties in photos:** the sender uploads the partner's photos, so require `create.photos.consent` (a confirmation that the people shown agreed). Offer the recipient a one-tap "Gỡ ảnh của tôi" (remove my photo) request on the gift page.
  - **Children:** Vietnamese rules treat under-16s specially and require parental consent (plus the child's own consent from age 7). Gate creation with `privacy.age` (16+). Do not market couple themes to minors.
- **Data-subject rights:** withdraw consent, access/export, correct, delete (`privacy.withdraw/export/delete`). Delete within **72 hours** of a request (the Decree 13 timeline; `privacy.deleteConfirm`), with no way to restore afterwards.
- **Minimisation and retention:**
  - Booth photos stay on-device unless the user saves them into a gift.
  - Gifts auto-expire. Default 90 days, user-adjustable (`create.expiry.label`, `privacy.retention`).
  - Strip EXIF/GPS from uploads server-side.
  - Gift URLs use ≥ 128-bit random IDs (unguessable), with optional secret question. Use `noindex` and no public gallery or feed. This also keeps the product clear of "social network" licensing obligations under Vietnam's internet-services decree; verify with counsel.
- **Filings:**
  - Prepare a **processing impact assessment dossier** (hồ sơ đánh giá tác động xử lý dữ liệu cá nhân).
  - If data is stored or processed outside Vietnam (e.g. a Singapore cloud), prepare a **cross-border transfer impact dossier**.
  - Under Decree 13, both were filed with the Ministry of Public Security's cybersecurity department (A05) within 60 days of starting processing. Confirm the current form and timing under the 2025 Law.
- **Breach:** notify the authority within **72 h**, and keep an incident runbook.
- **Data localisation:** Decree 53/2022/NĐ-CP (Cybersecurity Law) requires *domestic* enterprises in covered service categories to store certain Vietnamese users' data in Vietnam. If the operating company is Vietnamese, plan a VN-region primary or replica (Viettel IDC, VNG Cloud, FPT Cloud, CMC, BizFly) and confirm applicability with counsel.
- **Security:**
  - TLS everywhere, encryption at rest, signed short-lived media URLs.
  - Voice notes stored as Opus/AAC, never transcribed.
  - Admin access is logged.
  - Payment data is never stored (tokens and order IDs only).

### 5.6 Hosting and latency
- **Origin:** the Singapore region (e.g. AWS `ap-southeast-1`, GCP `asia-southeast1`, Vercel `sin1`, Supabase `ap-southeast-1`).
  - Typical round trips from Hà Nội/TP.HCM are tens of milliseconds (roughly 30-60 ms) in normal conditions. That is an estimate; measure with RUM.
  - Vietnam's international bandwidth depends on a few submarine cables (AAG, APG, IA, AAE-1, ADC…), and cable faults happen several times a year. During incidents, latency and loss to Singapore can spike for weeks.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Use a CDN with **PoPs inside Vietnam** (Cloudflare, AWS CloudFront and several regional CDNs run Hà Nội/TP.HCM edges; confirm on your plan) for HTML shells, fonts, sprites and gift media. Cache gift media at the edge with signed URLs + long TTLs.
  - Self-host the Vietnamese font subsets (one fewer origin).
  - Pre-render the gift shell statically. Fetch the gift JSON (≈ a few KB) from the edge cache, and invalidate it on edit.
  - Keep the first act playable from cache alone.
  - Use **AVIF/WebP** for sprites (with `<picture>` fallback) and **Opus** for voice notes, with an AAC fallback for iOS < 17.
  - Upload photos with resumable/chunked requests and client-side downscaling (≤ 2048 px, JPEG 0.85) before upload. This halves bytes on 4G.
- **If data residency in VN is required:** run storage/DB in a VN cloud region, with a CDN in front and Singapore only as a stateless compute fallback.

## 6. QA checklist (localisation-specific)
1. Every string in `vi.json` passes `build_vi.py`: NFC, vi/en key parity, placeholder parity, and pronoun placeholders only in sender-voice keys.
2. Render each pronoun set × tone for all `gift.*` strings and have a native speaker review them. Check especially "mình" (we) vs "mình" (I) in the `minh-ban` set, where the sender-voice copy uses "chúng mình" for "we".
3. Diacritic torture test in every font/role: "Ừ, Ặng, Ễ, Ỗ, ượu, ỹ, gặp, Nguyễn Thị Ánh Dương, ĐẶNG". No clipping in pills, stamps, the print slot, or canvas strips.
4. Typewriter with NFD input (paste "Nguyễn" decomposed): no detached marks, correct grapheme count.
5. Open a gift link from Zalo (iOS + Android) and Messenger (iOS + Android), plus Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet. Run the booth through the full fallback ladder in each.
6. Low-end Android (2 GB RAM): a 4-shot strip composes in under 2 s and saves via long-press.
7. Payments: a sandbox order for each of VietQR/MoMo/ZaloPay/VNPay, including webview deeplink failure → QR, app-switch return, duplicate IPN, and expiry.
8. A map at country zoom shows Hoàng Sa / Trường Sa correctly and labels "Biển Đông".
9. No banned motifs (§2.4) in any frame, sticker or OG image. B&W single-photo frames are never black-bordered.
10. Lunar dates for the next 3 years match `amlich.py` output (Tết 2027 = 6/2, Thất Tịch 2027 = 8/8, Trung Thu 2027 = 15/9).
