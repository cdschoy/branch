# v2b — "Let You Break My Heart Again" lyric video, second half (frames 795–1676)

Source: 1012×576, 30 fps (a screen recording/re-encode with irregular frame pacing). Range: 26.50 s – 55.87 s.
Merged from analyst segments v2-D (795–905), v2-E (900–1262) and v2-F (1255–1676), with verifier corrections applied.
Rules used: corrected values override the analyst's, refuted events are dropped, missed events are added, and overlaps are deduped.
Frame → seconds: t = frame / 30. Timing values in ms use 33.3 ms per frame.

## 1. Summary

The second half runs as three long, music-locked shots, joined by two very different transitions.

1. **Dreamy descent (795–870).** A parallax scroll down through pink-graded photographic clouds.
   - Clouds rise at about 3.6 px/frame.
   - Polaroids rise at 3.8–4.0 px/frame and swing slowly counter-clockwise.
   - Translucent heart balloons rise on interleaved depth planes.
   - A flat magenta silhouette of a falling girl drops through the frame in about 2 s. Her speed rises in three stepped plateaus and she rotates 10.5° clockwise.
   - A static lilac/grey vignette sits on top of everything.
   - The previous 3D headline "YOU." drifts up-left and is **hard-cut** at 808 while still partly on screen.
2. **45° hard-edged linear wipe (871–877, about 205 ms).** It reveals a top-down cel-shaded picnic flat-lay with a pink gingham cloth, a teacup and a lattice cherry pie.
3. **Picnic camera tour (877–1235).** A virtual camera moves over one large world canvas.
   - Lyrics sit in world space, typed glyph by glyph at about 50 ms per glyph.
   - Camera moves, in order: a tilt-down (about 285 px), a whip-pan right (about 918 px), and a swing-back left (+555 px) combined with an 8.6% zoom-out and a −6.3° roll.
   - An anime hand slides in a polaroid of the real couple. Its caption types word by word on the beats.
   - The mug and pie spin as counter-rotating turntables and ease to a stop together.
   - The camera then "breathes": a zoom-out followed by an ease-in push-in.
4. **"Focus on memory" match transition (1235–1261).**
   - Push-in ×1.56 and straighten by +15°.
   - The background defocuses.
   - The caption pixel-glitches out.
   - An ease-out cross-dissolve lands on a POV close-up: two hands holding the polaroid over bokeh.
5. **POV close-up (1261–1676).** Everything here is layered ambient motion.
   - A two-sine handheld sway, with background parallax at about 2×.
   - A cursive "love u" writes on, then gets struck through in pink.
   - A 3-line lyric types per glyph inside the photo: "THEN, OF COURSE, / I'LL LET YOU BREAK / MY HEART AGAIN", in sizes 1 : 1.3 : 1.8.
   - A glowing pen spark draws a neon coil "phone-cord" between the couple.
   - Five petals fall.
   - Rain/tear droplets land as lenses, slide down stick-slip and wash out the letters they cross.
   - The section ends on a diagonal page-wipe corner (1672) and a hard cut to the outro (1677).

Across the whole range nothing overshoots. The motion is calm, eased and beat-aware, at 152 BPM (one beat ≈ 11.84 frames ≈ 395 ms).

## 4. Reusable motion primitives

1. **Parallax descent field.** Use one scroll clock with per-layer factors. Clouds 1.0 (108 px/s at 1012 w ≈ 10.7% of width per second). Cards 1.1×, with each card also slowly rotating ±6–12° over 2–3 s, linear, at a random phase. Near balloons 1.2–1.4×. Headline 2.6× vertically plus a lateral drift. Use linear velocity throughout, no easing.
2. **Hero fall-through.** A flat silhouette crosses the frame in about 2 s with an ease-in (CSS `ease-in` or bezier (0.333,0.191,0.667,0.525)) and a linear 10° CW rotation. It ignores the camera sway.
3. **Hard lyric out-point.** The headline is removed in a single frame (`autoAlpha:0`) while still partly visible. No fade.
4. **45° hard wipe.** A straight edge at a fixed 45° travels from the top-right corner to the bottom-left in about 205 ms, linear, with no feather. The incoming scene starts washed out (brightness about 1.05, saturate about 0.88) and settles over 270 ms with easeInOutSine.
5. **Instant-glyph typewriter (scene lyrics).** A word starts at its vocal timestamp, about 2–4 frames before the beat. Each glyph steps to full opacity with a 50 ms stagger, with no fade and no cursor.
6. **Soft typewriter (photo lyrics).** Each glyph fades in over 67 ms, linear, with a 67–100 ms stagger. Lines grow in size, 1 : 1.3 : 1.8. The text is clipped to the photo window.
7. **Word-by-word caption.** Whole words pop one per beat (about 395 ms), suited to a personal message.
8. **World-space flat-lay camera.** A single world container is animated through x, y, scale and rotation, with one eased move per lyric phrase. Measured moves: tilt 2.6 s (0.65,0,0.48,0.88); whip 1.27 s easeInOutQuad at peak about 1.4 screen-widths/s with no blur; swing-back 2.3 s (0.33,0,0.41,1) combined with −8.6% scale and −6.3° roll, all with transform-origin on the hero card.
9. **Lazy-susan props.** Props counter-rotate at ±30°/s, linear. They ease out together (easeOutQuad, about 1.6 s) after the camera lands.
10. **Hand presents polaroid.** The card slides about 650 px diagonally up-left in world space, easeOutCubic or spring (k127, c31), settling in about 1.1 s. Its rotation runs −29° → −16.5° on a long expo-out (about 2.5 s+). It counter-rotates against the camera roll.
11. **Breathing anchored zoom.** Idle −10% over 3.2 s with sine in-out, then +13% over 1.6 s ease-in, with the origin on the hero card.
12. **Focus-memory transition.** Push ×1.56, straighten +15°, and +100 px x over 867 ms ease-in-out. Blur the background to 12 px with ease-in. Glitch the caption out over 200 ms. Cross-dissolve to the close-up over 270–330 ms ease-out, then settle with ease-out (spring k207, c28).
13. **POV handheld sway.** A pure translate built from a sum of incommensurate sines (≈0.9% and 0.45% of width in x, 0.5% in y). The background moves about 2× in phase.
14. **Pen write-on and neon doodle.** An SVG dash draw at constant speed with a glowing spark that pops on, follows `getPointAtLength`, and is removed instantly.
15. **Petal particle.** Constant velocity of about 10–12 px/frame down and 0–10 px/frame left (roughly 330 px/s), a 3D tumble with a period of about 0.8–1 s, a gentle x flutter, and the top z-index. Spawn about every 1–3.5 s near beats.
16. **Rain-on-photo droplet.** A faint fall streak with a cone head for about 5–8 frames. It lands as a 45 px lens instantly, with no pop. It holds for 200–400 ms, then slides stick-slip (10–18 px jumps over 100 ms). Its trail permanently blurs the text it crosses.

### Illustration and texture

The art mixes two registers.

## 8. Pacing and audio sync

The tempo is 152 BPM (395 ms per beat).

- **Caption words lock to beats** within ±4 frames.
- **The girl's first speed step** (833/834) falls about 1 frame before beat 835. The second step (841/842) and the "YOU." cut (808) are off-beat.

- **Energy curve**: dreamy constant drift, then a sharp wipe, then long eased camera phrases, then a calm hold, then a fast focus push, then a quiet ambient close-up that slowly "breaks" (droplets wash the lyric). The music fades about 1640–1676. The section ends on a near-still hold and a hard cut.
