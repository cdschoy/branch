Source: 1016x576 screen recording, 30 fps, frames 1–817 (0.00–27.20 s). f818+ = TikTok outro (ignored). TikTok watermark jumps TL→BR at f150/151 (artefact). Capture has variable frame pacing (duplicate frames, ±50% per-frame velocity jitter) — curves below are fitted on cumulative positions.

## 1. Summary

A two-tap opening ritual followed by a content page:

1. **Landing**: flat cream (#f0e7d6) screen, small watercolor kraft envelope (≈13.6% vw) with a 2.5 px idle float, "Tap to open" in Dancing Script below.
2. **Tap 1** (f32): 233 ms wobble, prompt linear fade 133 ms, then at +400 ms a closed→open artwork crossfade that dips through cream (333 ms).
3. **Bloom burst** (f58–156, 3.3 s): painted peony/rose/lily sprites spawn translucent at the envelope centre and push radially out until full-bleed (coverage easeOut). Edges settle asynchronously (top f139, right f150, left f156).
4. **Hold**: flower wall drifts down non-rigidly at ≈72 px/s; "Tap to reveal" fades in at exactly +4.0 s after tap 1.
5. **Tap 2** (f189): prompt cut, wall falls with **ease-in** (never decelerates, ≈99% vh by f227), contrast-washes toward its mean colour, and the overlay clears with a top-to-bottom sweep + abrupt unmount at f235 (1.53 s after tap). Page beneath is pre-mounted and static.
6. **Songs for you** mixtape page: cream paper column over a watercolor room; sage cassette whose reels counter-rotate (1.09–1.10 s/rev linear) once media fires `playing`; track buttons with hover scale 1.06 and a 150 ms ease-out active-style crossfade.
7. **Heart FAB** (f405) opens a non-modal popover drawer: slides from fully off-canvas (≈−290 px) over 570 ms cubic-bezier(.18,1,.49,.98) with a faster 320–370 ms ease-out fade and a heavy drop shadow; inner scroll reveals letter → voice note → bouquet → Spotify → YouTube.
8. YouTube embed: load, play, native fullscreen, exit (reflow bug: scroll 100→41, card inner scroll reset), pause. Reels keep spinning after pause (state desync — anti-pattern).

Motion character: tiny, soft UI micro-motion (100–370 ms, linear/ease-out) contrasted with one lavish 3 s floral spectacle and a curtain-drop exit where three properties overlap with offsets. Floating chrome (heart FAB + pill, Help) always sits above transition overlays.

## 4. Motion primitives

1. **Idle float**: `translateY(-2.5px ↔ 0)`, 3 s ease-in-out infinite. Cancelled on tap; y returns to 0 over 100 ms ease-out.
2. **Tap wobble**: `rotate: [0, -2, 1.9, -1]deg`, 233 ms, times [0, .33, .62, 1], ease-in-out. The element keeps the −1° rest tilt afterwards (a "handled" feel). No scale.
3. **Prompt fade-out**: opacity 1→0, 133–150 ms linear, starting 1 frame after the tap.
4. **Artwork state crossfade with paper dip**: both layers start together. The outgoing layer uses easeOutQuad and the incoming layer ease-in, over 333 ms. The sum dips to ~56% mid-way, so the paper shows through.
5. **Bloom burst**: a radial sprite emitter at the hero centre. Each sprite fades in over 150 ms, scales 0.35→1 and translates radially with cubic-bezier(.17,.06,.19,.6) over 0.9–1.4 s, newest on top and translucent. Overall coverage follows cubic-bezier(.16,.53,.66,.97) over ~2.4 s to full-bleed. Settle the edges at staggered times, with a final edge-biased wave.
6. **Ambient layer drift**: wall layers drift down at 1.3–3 px/f (mean 72 px/s) with slight lateral parallax, linear.
7. **Timed prompt**: fade in over 200 ms ease-out at a fixed +4.0 s after the trigger tap, to ~0.85 opacity with a soft text-shadow. Remove it instantly on tap.
8. **Curtain-drop exit** (3 overlapping properties): translateY 0→105vh, ease-in (power1.in), ~1.4 s; contrast wash (crossfade to a pre-blurred or flat mean-colour copy), 700 ms near-linear from +33 ms; opacity ease-in plus a top-to-bottom mask sweep from +0.9 to +1.53 s, then unmount. The destination is pre-mounted.
9. **Hover grow**: scale 1.06 on small round buttons and 1.04 on the 35 px FAB, 130–200 ms ease-out. No colour tint and no press scale.
10. **Active-style swap**: background, icon colour and border change over 150 ms ease-out on both buttons simultaneously. The pressed ring is applied instantly on the click frame.
11. **Three-tier media state**: selected (instant) → Starting (text) → Playing (icon, text and reels on the media `playing` event).
12. **Reel loop**: rotate 360° in 1.1 s linear infinite. The right reel runs in reverse (mirror). An optional ~2 px off-centre origin gives a toy wobble.
13. **Anchored drawer**: translateX(calc(-100% - 19px))→0 over 570 ms cubic-bezier(.18,1,.49,.98), with opacity over 320–370 ms easeOutQuad (solid before the glide finishes). Heavy shadow `0 16px 44px rgba(20,8,12,.6)`; no backdrop dim.
14. **Instant chrome swaps**: status text, hint-pill removal and icon glyphs change with 0 ms.

## 7. Interaction flow
1. Land → envelope floats; the heart FAB + "There's more here" pill appear on the first pointer move (f17).
2. Tap envelope → wobble, prompt fade, open crossfade, bloom burst (non-interruptible, 4.0 s to the next prompt).
3. Tap anywhere on the wall → curtain-drop reveal (1.53 s) → Songs page (pre-mounted).
4. Hover a track → scale. Click → instant "Starting:" plus the active swap. On media `playing`: pause icon, "Playing:", reels spin.
5. Click the heart FAB → pill removed; letter drawer slides in (non-modal, page stays live, music continues).
6. Scroll inside the drawer: letter → voice note (0:15) → bouquet → Spotify → YouTube (hard stop, no bounce).
7. YouTube: click → spinner → play → native fullscreen → exit (reflow bug) → pause (reels keep spinning: desync).
8. Page scroll reveals the "Alex" signature + FINISH MIXTAPE CTA (no reveal animation, no hover state, arrow cursor).

Build recommendations:
- Use `cursor:pointer` on all tap targets, including the full-screen wall.
- Add a pulsing hint on prompts.
- Debounce taps during choreographed sequences.
- Bind the reels and track state to real player events (play / pause / waiting).
- Use an in-app theater overlay instead of native fullscreen.
- Avoid fixed-height inner-scroll cards for media.
- Keep the chrome z-order: chrome > overlay > page.

## 8. Pacing and audio
- Audio is a TikTok overlay at 161.5 BPM (≈11.1 f/beat). Every alignment is coincidental: clicks are human-timed and the media latency is network-bound.
- Choreographed stage offsets from tap 1:
  - wobble 0
  - prompt fade +33 ms
  - crossfade +400 ms
  - seed +870 ms
  - envelope gone +1.17 s
  - full-bleed ≈+3.4 s
  - settled +4.13 s
  - prompt +4.0 s
- Reveal: 1.53 s ≈ 4 beats. Crossfade 333 ms ≈ 1 beat. Burst ≈ 6.5–9 beats.
- Rhythm: short holds (3–5 frames) between stages make the sequence read as discrete steps.
- Suggestion: quantise stage delays to a chosen track's beat (~370 ms) and keep UI micro-motion at 100–370 ms.
