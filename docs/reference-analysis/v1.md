# v1 — "Emma & Liam booth" web photo-booth gift: merged motion and style report

**Source:** a 1024×576 screen recording, 644 frames at 30fps (21.47s). The capture was **25fps upsampled to 30fps**: every 6th frame is a duplicate (3, 9, 15 … 249 … 426 … 510 …), so every timing is quantized to about 40ms (±1 frame). Ignore the TikTok watermark, its logo glitch (it jumps from bottom-left to bottom-right at f150/151) and the TikTok music card (f543).
**Merge method:** I merged four segment analyses (A 1–101, B 96–250, C 244–430, D 424–644) with their adversarial verifications. Where the analyst and verifier disagreed, the verifier's corrected values are used. Refuted events are dropped (v1B-e17, the "ambient heart under sign"). Missed events are added (A-m01 coin ping, A-m02 booth flash glow, B-m01 "There's more here" pill removal, D-M1 TikTok card). Events seen in two overlapping segments are deduplicated (strip-1 print, letter open, letter close and scrim, pill exit).

## 1. Summary

The page is one illustrated scene: a painted pink photo booth against a hand-painted striped wall with a lilac checker floor. Crisp flat web UI floats over it (cream pills, a plum media card, a lined letter). The recording runs one full gift loop:

1. **Idle.** The coin pings, the curtain sways, and the status pill reads "drop a coin ✦".
2. **Coin tap.** A heart token drops into the slot and the name tags fade out.
3. **Countdown.** A stamp-pop "3-2-1" bubble.
4. **Capture.** Four flash ticks. Each has a white flash, a booth-centred glow, a 1–3px shake of the booth group, a painted starburst, and a burst of leg/dog animation.
5. **Printing.** "printing..." then three photo strips print linearly from the slot. Each **tosses** with a scale overshoot, an arc and a tilt into a fanned layout. A caption fades in and confetti fires on each landing.
6. **Prompt.** The pill changes to "tap a strip ✦". A heart burst plays on the centre strip.
7. **Music card.** It slides in from the right while the "There's more here" pill vanishes.
8. **Letter.** Tapping a strip opens a plum scrim and a love letter (fade, 12px rise, scale .965→1). The media rail stays live above the scrim: voice note, bouquet, YouTube "our song" and Memory map.
9. **Close.** The letter closes as a mirror of the open.
10. **Next chapter.** Tapping the rose triggers a **full-screen falling-flower curtain wipe** (about 1.4s) that hides a reset to the empty booth. The curtain then settles with a damped pendulum swing.

The motion language: **snappy stamps for the booth mechanics, soft 300ms fades for the reading UI, linear physical motion for the printer and the flowers, and spring overshoot (about 5–6%) only on the strip toss.** Nothing bounces more than about 6%.

## 4. Reusable motion primitives

1. **Stamp pop** (countdown digits, any "beat" badge). Mount at full opacity, scale 1.12, spring k 3000 / c 50. That gives about 1.05 on frame 1, a 2.5–3% dip, and settles in 100ms. Exit is opacity only over 50–70ms linear. Cadence: 280ms per number in the reference. Use about 800–1000ms for a real camera.
2. **Camera flash tick.** A white layer (below the HUD) plus a radial glow centred on the subject: opacity .9 → 0 linear over about 150ms, ≤1-frame rise. Same tick: shake only the subject group (≤2.5px, 2–3 random steps over about 100ms, `steps(1)`), a painted starburst (52–56px at 1024w: scale .7 → 1 → 1.05, rotate +12°, life 200ms) and 2–3 twinkles. Swap content under the peak. Ticks are 240ms apart.
3. **Slot print.** The mini item (scale .29) translates linearly inside an `overflow:hidden` slot at about 95–160px/s (400–700ms).
4. **Toss-to-slot.** Keyframes over 370–600ms. Scale .29 → 1.056 → 1 (spring k≈150–370, ζ≈.6). Arc: y rises 26–51px above the target with ease-out, then drops with ease-in into a hard stop. x uses easeOutQuad. Tilt goes to ±16°·sign(dx) at about 60% of the duration, then unwinds to a resting −3.6° / +1.3° / +4°. Newest on top.
5. **Land reward.** Caption fade (opacity only, about 230ms `ease`, +33ms after landing) plus a confetti fountain (about 15 flakes, rise 30px, gravity fall 100px, 800ms), rendered in a stage overlay rather than clipped to the photo.
6. **Heart burst.** 13–15 hearts pop and float up about 70px with ease-out, fading over the last 300ms. Total 1.1s. Used as an attention cue when the next step is unlocked.
7. **Dock slide-in.** x +100% → 0 with cubic-bezier(.27,.85,.27,.98) over 610ms, opacity `ease` over 430ms. No overshoot.
8. **Modal open/close.** Scrim, letter and side chips share one 300ms `ease` opacity timeline. The card adds y 12 → 0 and scale .965 → 1 over 170ms (close: 130ms, mirrored). Static −1.5° tilt.
9. **Instant state label.** Pill text swaps with no tween and the width snaps to the content.
10. **Instant teardown + covering wipe.** Remove the old UI in ≤1 frame, start the decorative wipe 60ms later, swap the scene underneath at ≥70% cover (100–130ms crossfade), and the new scene needs no entrance animation.
11. **Falling-flower curtain.** Sprites use three nested transforms: linear y fall at 1.0–2.5 vh/s, a sine x sway (35–50px, 280–400ms), and a constant spin (150–300°/s). No scale, opacity, shadow or blur changes.
12. **Pendulum ambient.** rotate ±1° about the top-centre origin, period about 2.2–2.35s, asymmetric. After an impulse: a damped spring k 46 / c 3.8.
13. **Hotspot ping.** A ring expands from 1 to 1.8 scale with opacity .7 → 0 over about 330ms, repeating. Optional sparkle shimmer pulses at random 0.3–1.5s intervals.

## 5. Transitions

- **Coin → countdown (f24–30):** the tap gives instant feedback (heart token and pill swap), then a 200ms pause before the first digit.
- **Capture → print (f82–90):** 233ms until "printing...", then 200ms more before the strip starts feeding. This is an anticipation beat; generate the composited strip here.
- **Print → prompt (f198–210):** 167ms until "tap a strip ✦" and the heart cue, plus 233ms until the music dock replaces the "There's more here" pill.
- **Gallery → letter (f242–252):** the hint clears instantly, then after 67ms the scrim, letter and chips fade in over 300ms. The media rail and rose stay above the scrim.
- **Letter → gallery (f422–431):** an exact mirror over 300ms.
- **Gallery → reset booth, "flower curtain" (f471–514):** instant teardown, then a 20% botanical veil, then the flower wipe (the leading edge crosses in 467ms, about 133ms of full cover, the trailing edge clears in about 630ms). A 100–133ms crossfade swap happens under the cover. Clean at +1433ms. A curtain damped swing follows.

### Typography (with Vietnamese-capable substitutes)
- **Sign "Emma & Liam booth", name tags, status pill:** a slanted monoline marker (Caveat SemiBold/Bold-like). The pill text is a rougher brush (Caveat Brush-like). Caveat and Caveat Brush have **no reliable Vietnamese subset**, so use **Pangolin, Itim, Mali, Sriracha or Playpen Sans**. Test ể / ờ / ậ / ữ.
- **Letter greeting/body:** a slanted heavy brush-pen hand with a distinctive long "s" (the face is unconfirmed). VN: **Playpen Sans / Mali / Pangolin**, body line-height about 17.5px, locked to the ruled lines.
- **Captions and chips:** an upright rounded casual hand (Patrick Hand / Gaegu). VN: **Patrick Hand** (has VN) or **Itim**.
- **Eyebrows ("EMMA & LIAM BOOTH", "MEMORY MAP"):** tracked caps (+0.12em), rounded sans or hand caps. VN: **Nunito / Be Vietnam Pro**.
- **Media card titles:** a bold transitional serif (Lora / Fraunces). VN: **Lora, Playfair Display, Fraunces** (check the subset).
- **Tooltip:** italic serif. VN: **Cormorant Garamond Italic / EB Garamond Italic / Lora Italic**.
- **"There's more here":** semibold sans. VN: **Be Vietnam Pro 600**.
- **Glyph motif:** the ✦ sparkle used inline.

### Illustration and texture
Procreate-style gouache/watercolor: brushy stripe edges, paper grain, watercolor-bleed floor, painted shading with no CSS shadows on the art. The characters are shown only from the knees down (boots, sneakers, ruffle socks) with a dachshund and a handbag, which personalises the booth without faces. Strip frames are painted dark wood with folk tulips. The flower sprites are cut-outs with soft petals, no outline and no shadow. The UI is flat vector with soft warm shadows (about 0 2px 6px rgba(80,40,50,.15–.25)).

## 7. Interaction flow

1. Land on the booth. The coin ping invites a tap ("drop a coin ✦" / VN "thả xu ✦").
2. Tap the coin. The heart drops into the slot and the pill reads "smile!" ("cười lên!"). The 3-2-1 countdown plays, then 4 flash captures (in the self-photobooth, grab the camera frame on each tick).
3. "printing..." ("đang in..."). Three strips print and toss, each with a caption and confetti.
4. "tap a strip ✦" ("chạm vào dải ảnh ✦"). The heart cue plays and the music dock replaces "There's more here".
5. Tap a strip. The letter modal opens. The music rail stays usable (voice note, bouquet, song, map).
6. Close (× / Esc / scrim). Back to the gallery. The chips "the letter" (reopen) and "take another" (reshoot) persist.
7. Tap the rose ("Only tap when you're finished reading to continue"). The flower-curtain wipe plays, then the empty booth resets for the next chapter or loop.

## 8. Pacing and audio

- **Track:** "Perfect" by Ed Sheeran at about 86 bpm (beat about 697ms), added in the TikTok edit. The site's own music card is unplayed at first, and the later audio includes the YouTube embed.
- **No beat sync:** the countdown (280ms), flash ticks (240ms) and toss intervals (1.2 and 1.43s) do not lock to beats. The animation is driven by timers and taps.
- **Inferred click transient:** at the letter close (f421–423).
- **Recommended SFX:** a coin clink at the tap, a soft tick per digit, a shutter per flash, a printer whirr during each print, a pop or rustle at each landing, a paper rustle on letter open and close, and a rustle or chime about 60ms after the rose tap, peaking at full cover (+0.6s).
- **Pacing:** brisk mechanical bursts (0.1–0.6s), then long reading holds (the letter is open about 5.6s), with a single 1.4s showpiece transition. The capture cadence may be sped up for TikTok, so scale the countdown to about 1s per digit for a real camera while keeping the ratios.
- **Reduced motion:** drop the shake, cap the flash at about .4, replace the flower wipe with a 300ms crossfade, and stop the ambient loops.

## 10. Open questions and low-confidence items

- Real-time speed: the 25 → 30fps capture is solid, but whether TikTok sped it up is unknown. Ratios are reliable; absolute durations of the capture sequence may need ×1.5–3.5 for a real booth.
- Coin ping repeat period: only one occurrence (>0.8s period). It could also be a press ripple.
- True flash peak may reach .9–1.0 between samples.
- Leg/dog animation: discrete sprites versus a short video loop per shot.
- Strip 1 tosses faster (367ms) than strips 2–3 (600ms). It may be a config difference or capture lag.
- Music dock trigger: most likely the "There's more here" tap (frame-locked removal), but no cursor is visible. The strip tap and the letter close trigger are also inferred.
- Fonts are visual matches only. The letter's long-s hand is unidentified.
