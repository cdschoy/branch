# Framework: Vietnamese digital gift card + self-photobooth

**Status:** v1.1, 2026-11-12 (critic round 2 applied; see §10.2 errata 19-40). This is the master document. Build from it. New here? Start at §0.5.

**The two products:**
- **(A) Gift card.** A sender makes a gift and shares a link. The recipient (a partner) opens it: an envelope, box or booth reveal, then a love letter, photos, songs and memories.
- **(B) Self-photobooth.** A camera for yourself: countdown, flash, photo strips, frames and stickers, then download or share.

**How this document was made.** Five reference videos (web "digital gift" experiences and one lyric video) were analysed frame by frame at 30 fps. Analysts and adversarial verifiers measured them, and an auditor cross-checked the reports. Specialists then designed the motion, visual, UX, Vietnamese-localisation and architecture layers. Three architecture proposals were scored by three judges. Every number here traces to frame evidence written `vN fA-B` (video N, frames A to B at 30 fps; 1 frame = 33.3 ms) or to a report row ID such as `v4 B7`.

**IP rule (applies to every section).** Reproduce the *motion feel and visual language* only: curves, durations, choreography, density and the kinds of objects. Never copy the reference art, the reference names ("Emma & Liam", "Noah", "Songs for you", the English template strings, **including in translation**), any brand UI or its component set, or the licensed mascot seen in v3. All illustration is commissioned as original work, and every asset carries a provenance record (§7.7).

**String rule.** UI copy is quoted here as `key` ("value") from `i18n/vi.json`. `i18n/tools/check_framework_strings.py` fails CI when a quoted Vietnamese string has no key or the value has drifted.

## 0. TL;DR

**The style in one paragraph.** A painted, cut-out paper world (gouache and watercolour objects with no outlines and no CSS shadows on the art) sits on cream and blush grounds. Small, flat, warm UI chrome floats on top. Its darkest colour is plum, never black. In each scene exactly one object is alive and asks to be touched. Touching it answers on the same frame. A short beat of stillness follows, then one spectacle of 1.4-3.3 s, usually made of flowers. Flowers clear the screen at the start of the gift and flood it at the end, so the experience is bookended. After the spectacle the reader gets a still page they control: a letter typed glyph by glyph, polaroids dealt at a tilt, and a non-modal drawer of extras (voice note, songs, memory map). The ending is gated behind a "read it first" corner button. Motion is restrained. Large surfaces never overshoot, and small rewards overshoot by 6% at most. Things leave by accelerating away and arrive by settling. Physical things such as printers, falling flowers and tape reels move linearly. Status text changes instantly. Status pills and painted hand labels speak warm, lowercase, hand-lettered Vietnamese; buttons, errors and legal text use sentence case (§6.3).

**The 10 rules.**

1. **One living object per screen.** Only the tap target moves: at most one hint loop on it plus at most one slow ambient prop (≤±3°, period ≥0.8 s). Everything else is still. Evidence: v3 M04/M14 f272-384; v5 f1-31; audit 1.12.
2. **Same-frame answer, then a beat.** Respond within 1 frame (wobble, sprite swap, label swap), then hold 67-233 ms before the next stage. Evidence: v5 f32 and f40-43; v4 f127-135; v1 f24-30.
3. **A physical ritual gates every reveal.** Tap the envelope, pull the ribbon, drop the coin, bite the treat. Never a generic "Next". Evidence: v5 f32; v4 f114-133, f167-203; v1 f24.
4. **One spectacle per act, bracketed by micro-motion.** The spectacle runs 1.4-3.3 s; everything around it runs 67-370 ms. Evidence: v5 bloom 3.27 s (f58-156); v4 curtain 2.23 s (f4-71); v1 wipe 1.37 s (f473-513).
5. **The overshoot budget is 0 / 1.5 / 4 / 6%.** Measure it as the peak in final-size units. Drawers, docks, modals, lids, pages and camera moves never overshoot. Evidence: v4 B7 (landing spring refuted), v4 C3 1.012, v4 B8 1.04, v1 #23 1.056; audit 1.1.
6. **Exits accelerate away, entrances settle.** Micro exits (labels, prompts, knots, whip-offs) take 67-133 ms. Panel and modal exits take ≤60% of their entrance and use ease-in (or the exact mirror, for the letter). Spectacle exits accelerate and never decelerate. Entrances are ease-out, back or spring (100-280 ms). Evidence: v4 B4 67 ms, B5 133 ms vs B8; v5 A-e04 133 ms, A-e12; v2a B-03/B-04.
7. **Chrome hard-cuts; content eases.** Status pills, labels, glyphs and sprite swaps take 0 ms. Cards, letters and photos ease. Evidence: v1 #5/#21/#34; v4 A9, C8; v5 B-03/B-05.
8. **Preload, decode and pre-mount before every transition.** The destination sits decoded under the cover. Never reveal an undecoded image. Evidence: v3 M25-M26 (200 ms freeze, then late pops); v5 A-e12.
9. **Plum, never black; cream, never white.** Darks are plum/wine/cocoa, scrims are plum with no blur, and paper is tinted. Evidence: v1 scrim rgba(70,25,55,.5); v4 drawer #49223D→#120415; v2b grade floor #7F436B.
10. **Vietnamese first, in verified fonts only.** User text renders only in the 20 fonts with verified 74/74 Vietnamese glyph coverage. Text is NFC, split by grapheme, and given diacritic-safe line heights and clip padding. The product works inside the Zalo and Messenger webviews. Evidence: `docs/reference-analysis/metrics/fonts_vietnamese.txt`; vietnam.md §4-§5.

## 1. What the references do

**Source-quality caveats (read before trusting a number).** v1 and v4 are 25 fps captures upsampled to 30, so their timings are ±1 source frame (about 40 ms). v2 has stalls and hitches, so only plateau speeds and cumulative-position fits are used from it. v3 is a phone filming an iPad, so its colours are camera-shifted; only the white-balance-corrected "True" values are used, as hue hints. v5 has ±50% per-frame velocity jitter, so fits use cumulative positions. Only v2 (152 BPM) is genuinely beat-locked. The v1, v3, v4 and v5 soundtracks (86.1 / 92.3 / 76.0 / 161.5 BPM, `summary.json`) are TikTok overlays. **Adopt durations and ratios, never absolute timestamps** (audit §0).

### 1.1 v1: photo-booth gift (`reference-analysis/v1.md`)
One illustrated scene: a painted pink photo booth against an uneven striped wall with a lilac checker floor, with crisp flat UI over it. The recipient drops a coin. A heart token falls into the slot. A stamp-pop 3-2-1 countdown plays, then four flash ticks. Three photo strips print linearly from the slot, and each tosses with an arc, a tilt and a 5.6% scale overshoot into a fanned gallery. Tapping a strip opens a love letter over a plum scrim while the media rail (voice note, bouquet, song, memory map) stays live above it. A gated rose button triggers a full-screen falling-flower wipe that hides a reset, and the booth curtain then settles with a damped pendulum swing.

Signature details:
- Stamp digit mounts at scale 1.12, dips 2.3-2.8% and settles in 100 ms; the exit is opacity only (f30-54).
- Layered flash tick: white peak .77-.85, a booth-centred glow to .93-.96, a ≤2.5 px stepped shake of the booth group only, and a painted starburst above the flash (f54-82).
- Strips print linearly through an `overflow:hidden` slot at 95-162 px/s (adopted ≈100 px/s, audit 1.10; tokens use 0.9 mini-strip heights/s). The toss lasts 367 ms for strip 1 (from scale .29) and 600 ms for strips 2-3 (from .36), always peaking at 1.056 → 1, with a ±16° tilt peak and rest tilts −3.6° / +1.3° / +4° (#22-#31, f90-198).
- Confetti on landing is not clipped to the strip (#25).
- The letter opens with opacity over 300 ms, y +12→0 and scale .965→1 over 170 ms, a static −1.5° tilt; the close is the exact mirror (f244-252, f422-431).
- Flower wipe: linear fall at about 2 viewport heights per second, 35-50 px sway, 150-300°/s spin, scene swapped at ≥70% cover (f473-513).
- Warning: the capture cadence is TikTok-fast (digits 280 ms apart, flashes 240 ms apart), which breaks WCAG 2.3.1. Do not copy it (audit A1).

### 1.2 v2a / v2b: lyric video (`reference-analysis/v2a.md`, `v2b.md`)
A beat-locked (152 BPM) lyric experience. **v2a:** large orchid script lyrics over pastel clouds. Each line enters with blur+fade or a per-glyph mosaic sweep, holds while slowly dollying and rolling (+1.5-5%/s scale, ±2-2.5°/s roll), then flies past the camera with ease-in, in a different direction each time. A grid-paper notepad types "SOME ♥ DAY," one glyph per sixteenth note. Polka-dot cards and extruded 3D words rise in depth-tiered parallax while petals fall. **v2b:** a parallax descent through pink clouds with rising polaroids. A 205 ms hard 45° wipe opens onto a gingham picnic flat-lay toured by a virtual camera (tilt, whip-pan, swing-back with zoom and roll). A focus-push match-cut lands on a POV close-up of hands holding a polaroid, with handheld sway, a cursive write-on, per-glyph lyrics inside the photo, and rain-drop lenses.

Signature details:
- Text is music-locked; ambient layers run free (v2a §8).
- Nothing overshoots in the whole video (v2b §1).
- `livingHold`: a hero card never freezes (v2a A-E16).
- Fly-through exits with keyframed opacity [1, 1, .15, 0] (v2a B-04 f200-206).
- Petals in 3 depth tiers, 1-3 on screen (v2a A-E04).
- Polaroid bottom border ≈21% of height (v2b §6).
- Pink-duotone photo grade with blacks lifted to no darker than #7F436B (v2b §6).
- Use this video only for an optional "song chapter" (C8). Its stalls, hitches and haze snaps are recording artifacts.

### 1.3 v3: storybook birthday gift filmed on an iPad (`reference-analysis/v3.md`)
A calm, flat, scrapbook-style site built from five browser-history pages: headline → "wanna see it?" yes/no → "tap the cake" → gift photo → love-letter collage. It has exactly four kinds of motion: a time-anchored typewriter, 140-270 ms opacity-only page crossfades (M06 140-170 in, M05 ≈250, M10 213-267, M21/M22 233), raster pops, and one irregular idle wobble on the tappable object, which is the only thing that moves. **The mascot is a licensed character. Use its placement as a layout lesson only, never the art.**

Signature details:
- Typewriter rule `msPerChar = min(127, 3220 / n)`, measured at 127 / 80.4 / 88.9 / 127 / 27.3 ms (M02, M08, M13, M19, M24). It fits 4 of 5 blocks: M13 (34 slots) is 6% faster than the 94.7 ms predicted (conf 0.65, audit 1.11). Glyphs are pre-laid-out and snap on with no caret and no fade. After jank the schedule catches up: the "Happy B" stall, then a flush (f514-522).
- Shared chrome stays pixel-stable across a crossfade.
- What to avoid: no press states, a 200 ms image-decode freeze with late photo pops (M25-M26), and no completion cue or end control (f605-669).

### 1.4 v4: "sweet box" gift (`reference-analysis/v4.md`)
A five-act ritual on a painted gingham tabletop:
1. A full-bleed watercolour flower collage falls away under gravity, revealing the wrapped box (f4-71).
2. The recipient pulls the ribbon. The bow tightens, the ribbon whips off with ease-in, and the lid flips open on one 280 ms easeInOutCubic curve while the box re-centres (f114-143).
3. A sticker pops (+4%), hearts and pennants appear, and six bite taps on three treats each swap a sprite and squash the box (f143-203).
4. The last bite auto-triggers the spread: the box slides left, the letter pops (+1.2%), and polaroids deal at a 150 ms stagger (f204-256). A plum voice-note drawer slides in with no dim (f310-325). A polaroid opens into a shared-element lightbox (f522-541).
5. The gated rose CTA throws a spinning rose. An impact flash, rays and a burst follow, then a flower wall rises at ≈250 vh/s (about 1400 px/s on the 576 px capture) and floods the screen (f570-610).

Signature details:
- Gravity curtain g ≈ 42 vh/s², with quadrant tilt and a linear 2 s fade (A1-A4).
- 800 ms trapezoid sway on one prop (A6).
- Hint ring ×1.84 over 800 ms (A7, r16 → 29.4; adopted ×1.8), plus an optional faster second pulse (A8, ×2.23).
- Glint chase shows the drag direction (A12).
- Lid flip with no landing spring (B7, the spring was refuted).
- Ease-in whip-off (B5).
- Caret notes that carry progress ("2 to go", B18). The note appears once per treat, so it counts treats remaining, not bites.
- Lightbox spring overshoot ≈5.35% (D6).
- Chrome hard-cuts in on timers (A9-A11).
- Bug to avoid: drawer and CTA stay tappable above the lightbox scrim (D5).

### 1.5 v5: envelope gift (`reference-analysis/v5.md`)
A two-tap opening, then a mixtape page:
1. A small watercolour kraft envelope floats 2.5 px on flat cream.
2. Tap 1: the envelope wobbles 233 ms, and the closed→open art crossfade dips through cream (333 ms, f44-54).
3. A bloom of painted peonies and roses spawns at the envelope centre and pushes radially to full-bleed over about 3.3 s. The edges settle asynchronously (f58-156).
4. "Tap to reveal" fades in at +4.0 s.
5. Tap 2: the flower wall falls with ease-in that never decelerates, washes toward its mean colour, and is masked away top-to-bottom. The pre-mounted page is revealed at +1.53 s (f189-235).
6. The songs page has a sage cassette whose reels counter-rotate at 1.1 s/rev on `playing`.
7. A heart FAB slides out a non-modal popover drawer over 570 ms on cubic-bezier(.18,1,.49,.98) (f405-422).

Signature details:
- Same-frame wobble that keeps a −1° "handled" tilt (A-e03).
- Paper-dip art swap (A-e05).
- Curtain drop as three overlapping properties (A-e12-14).
- Honest media status: selected → "Starting…" → "Playing…" only on the real `playing` event (B-03-B-06).
- Floating chrome above all overlays (A-e15).
- Bugs to avoid: reels keep spinning after pause (C-e13/C-e15); native fullscreen exit breaks scroll (f709-710); the prompt is on a fixed timer that fires before the burst settles (f152 vs f156).

## 2. Design principles

1. **Two registers, never mixed.** Painted illustration carries all texture, and its shading is painted in. UI chrome is flat, small, edge-anchored, and casts soft warm shadows. Never put a CSS shadow, outline or blur on the art. Never put paper grain over the painted art (visual.md §0, §4.1).
2. **Diegetic first.** The object is the button: envelope, ribbon, coin, treat, strip. One short lowercase hand label names the action (`gift.label.tapToOpen` ("chạm để mở")). A generic button appears only as an accessibility fallback, after 8 s (ux.md §7.3).
3. **Restraint is the luxury.** Small overshoots, few moving things, long stillness on reading surfaces. The spectacle feels big because everything around it is calm (G1, G2, audit 1.12).
4. **The recipient sets the pace.** Spectacles are timer-driven and short. Reading surfaces are user-paced and never auto-advance. Rituals auto-advance one frame after their goal is reached (v4 f203→f204), never on a timer that ignores the animation (audit A8).
5. **The truth in every indicator.** Status labels are state labels. Media indicators follow real player events. "Đang phát" appears only on `playing` (G16; v5 B-05 vs C-e15).
6. **Bookend with flowers.** The intro curtain and the finale flood use the same sprite family, and the finale flowers are slightly more saturated (v4 §6). Use one `FlowerCurtain` primitive with four modes (§4.2).
7. **Never trap, never dead-end.** Every scene has a forward path and a back path. Browser back never replays a spectacle. The end card offers replay, reply and "make your own" (ux.md §4.1; audit A9).
8. **The low-end Android phone inside Zalo is the primary device.** Design at 390×844 with a 360×780 floor, a 3 GB-RAM budget, in-app webviews, no hover, and a flaky connection to Singapore (vietnam.md §5).
9. **Private by construction.** Booth photos never leave the device unless the user sends them into a gift. Gift links are unguessable. The OG preview never shows content (ux.md §7).
10. **Culturally native, not touristy.** Vietnamese occasions, flowers, treats and pronouns drive the skins. Avoid the postcard tropes (§6.4).

## 3. Visual system

Source of truth: `design-tokens/design-tokens.json`, compiled to `design-tokens/tokens.css`. Rationale and full specs: `reference-analysis/visual.md`.

### 3.1 Using the tokens

- **Core colours** are `--c-<group>-<step>` (for example `--c-ink-800`, `--c-plum-panel-top`).
- **Theme slots** are `--t-<slot>`. A theme is applied with `data-theme="garden-envelope"` (or another theme) on a scene root. Components read **slots only** (`--t-bg`, `--t-surface`, `--t-ink`, `--t-display`, `--t-accent`, `--t-hint`, `--t-hint-ink`, `--t-fab`, `--t-panel-top`, `--t-panel-bottom`, `--t-scrim` ...). `:root` carries `booth-stripes`, so a theme that omits a slot (oli-notebook has no panel) inherits it. `build.py` rejects any theme colour that is pure `#FFFFFF`, near-black or a neutral grey (chroma under 4%).
- **Type styles** are `--type-<style>-{family,weight,size,line-height,tracking}`.
- **Motion** variables are `--d-*` (durations), `--e-*` (easings), `--spring-*` (CSS `linear()` springs) with `--spring-*-dur`, and `--amb-*` (ambient periods). On WebViews without `linear()` (before Chromium 113), `tokens.css` falls back each spring to its nearest bezier.
- **Never write a raw hex, bezier or duration in a component.** Lint for it (§7.2).

**Vietnamese typography rules (mandatory; visual.md §3.4, vietnam.md §4):**
1. **Line-height minimums.** Sans and serif ≥1.5 (headings included). Hand body 1.6-1.65. Script ≥1.7 (Great Vibes 1.75). All caps ≥1.3. Single-line labels ≥1.35. `build.py` asserts these per family class.
2. **Clip padding.** Any element with `overflow:hidden`, a mask, a clip-path or `background-clip:text` gets `padding-top: 0.25em` (script) or `0.18em` (caps/extruded). This covers the print slot, polaroid windows, pills and shimmer masks.
3. **Tracking.** Caps ≤0.15em. Never letter-space script. Never fake uppercase on script. Set `font-synthesis: none` on script and display faces, and never italicise script (use Playfair italic).
4. **Extrusion.** 3D text-shadow extrusion ≤0.06em. Test "ĐỢI ANH NHÉ".
5. **Optical centring in pills and stamps.** Use `padding-block: .42em .38em`.
6. **Minimum sizes.** 15 px body, 13 px captions and labels at a 360 px viewport (the same floor as §5.7; dot-below marks merge at 11-12 px on low-DPI Android).
7. **Line breaking.** Break at spaces with `hyphens: manual`. Use `text-wrap: balance` for headings and `pretty` for letters. Wrap each word in a `nowrap` span so a line never breaks inside a syllable.
8. **Loading.** Self-host the Vietnamese + Latin subsets. Preload exactly the scene plan's critical faces (above) and fetch the deferred ones during the first spectacle. Gate the first reveal on `document.fonts.load('400 20px Mali', 'ắệữ')`. The sample text must contain Vietnamese letters, or the Vietnamese `unicode-range` file never downloads.
9. **QA strings** (in Storybook): "Gửi người thương nhất — Chúc mừng sinh nhật!", "ĐỢI ANH NHÉ · Ễ Ặ Ỗ Ờ Ự", "Thương em nhiều lắm, đừng quên nụ cười này nhé.", "chạm vào dải ảnh ✦", "đang in…", "cười lên!", "Ừ, Ặng, Nguyễn Thị Ánh Dương, ĐẶNG".

**One duration rule for springs.** A choreography step that names `spring.X` lasts `spring.X.settleMs`, and the CSS twin runs `--spring-x` over `--spring-x-dur`. Putting a shorter step duration on the `linear()` curve compresses the physics (a 1400 ms pendulum would swing with a 0.39 s period instead of the measured 0.93 s), so `build.py` fails any spring step whose `dur` differs from `settleMs`. Later steps may start before the spring settles (the lightbox controls start at 430 ms, when the photo is visually home).

**Overshoot tiers.** Peaks are measured in final-size units, which is what the eye sees, and are checked against `peakFinal` only. The reports mixed "% of final size" with "% of travel". For example, 1.056 is 5.6% of final size but 7.9% of a .29→1 travel (audit 1.1).

#### C8. Lyric / song chapter (optional, beat-locked; v2a, v2b)
The clock is `audio.currentTime` of **our own licensed track** only. YouTube's `getCurrentTime()` is coarse (±50-100 ms) and fine for line entrances only. Lyric mode is not offered with Spotify. Never use setTimeout for beat-locked events.

```
beat = 60000 / BPM;  sixteenth = beat / 4
enter(k) = beatGrid(k.start)      hold(k) = quantise(k.len, [2, 4, 8] beats)
exit(k)  = enter(k+1) − 1 beat     gap = 0-200 ms of empty sky
chapter end = sceneTilt (y → −80 vh, 670 ms inQuad, hard stop) + 1 beat of empty sky (preload next) + hard cut on the downbeat
```

The worked example at 76 BPM (beat 789 ms) is in motion.md C8: sky crossfade one beat ahead, `textBlurIn`, `livingHold`, `flyThroughExit`, `glyphMosaicSweep`, `typewriterPop` at 197 ms per grapheme, `wordByWord` for the name. **Ambient layers are never beat-locked.**

### 4.6 Performance budgets and renderer choice

**Device tiers.**
- Initial tier (`motion.ts → initialTier()`): `hardwareConcurrency ≤ 4 || deviceMemory ≤ 3` → low; `hardwareConcurrency ≥ 8 && deviceMemory ≥ 6` → high; otherwise mid. **When `deviceMemory` is undefined** (Safari/iOS and Firefox expose it nowhere), start at mid explicitly rather than reading undefined as a number. Safari does expose `hardwareConcurrency`, possibly clamped for privacy, so M0 records what real iPhones report before the thresholds are trusted.
- **Runtime probe** (`probeTier()`): sample rAF deltas during the landing idle. If p90 > 20 ms over 30 frames, drop one tier *before* the first spectacle; if p90 < 12 ms over 60 frames, promote mid → high. Persist the result in `localStorage` (try/catch).
- Each spectacle reports `spectacle_frame_p90` and `dropped_frames`.
- A remote kill-switch can force the lite mechanic per device class.

### 5.2 Product A: sender creator flow

**Principles.**
- **Draft-first autosave.** IndexedDB immediately, the server debounced 1.5 s, both in try/catch. `common.draftSaved` ("Bản nháp đã lưu") is a 0 ms label.
- **The preview is the product.** A pinned 40% phone preview plays the real recipient player with a 10-minute signed draft token.
- **Minimum gift = theme + recipient name + letter.** Every other step has `common.skip` ("Bỏ qua").
- Ten steps in three groups, shown as 3 dots (`create.group.prepare` · `create.group.content` · `create.group.send`), never a 10-step bar and never a "step n of 10" counter. Steps 1-3 are *prepare*, 4-8 *content*, 9-10 *send*.

**Uploads.**
1. In a worker: `createImageBitmap(file, {imageOrientation:'from-image'})`.
2. Draw at long edge 2048 (thumbnail 480).
3. Encode WebP q.86 (JPEG q.88 fallback). This drops EXIF/GPS by construction.
4. Upload resumably with a per-photo progress ring and a non-blocking `common.retry` chip.
5. The server validates magic bytes, size and dimensions, and rejects any residual EXIF.

**Share.**
- Publish freezes a version and mints a ≥128-bit slug per channel (§7.6).
- Share sheet order: Web Share → Zalo → Messenger (`fb-messenger://share?link=`) → copy link → QR card (1080×1350 PNG for printing into a real card).
- After sharing, a status strip shows `create.status.sent` → `create.status.opened` (opt-in receipt) → `create.status.replied`. Edits are free until the first open, then `create.share.update` ("Gửi bản cập nhật") shows the recipient a `gift.updatedPill` ("Có cập nhật mới") pill.
- `create.share.revokeLink` ("Thu hồi liên kết này") revokes one channel's link; `create.share.revoke` withdraws the whole gift.

**Capture timing.**
- The C3 product column (`C3product`) applies. A tap during the countdown pauses it (`booth.pill.paused` ("tạm dừng · chạm để tiếp")). Taps during the last capture are ignored.
- **Screen flash (functional):** a `#FFF6EE` layer to 1.0 in ≤1 frame, held ≥120 ms. Grab the first `requestVideoFrameCallback` frame at or after +100 ms, then decay over 150 ms.
- **Flash-latency calibration:** measure the delay from flash rise to the first lit frame on each device and persist it. Shift the grab by that delay, and fall back to a fast mode if it is unstable.
- **Decorative flash (flash off):** P23, with capture at the flash start.
- The web cannot raise screen brightness, so say so honestly in QA.
- `ImageCapture.takePhoto()` is not the default. It stalls the preview 300-1000 ms on many low-end cameras. Use it only behind a high-tier feature test.

  | Polaroid | 1080×1312 |
  | Story | 1080×1920 |
  | `booth.layout.twoUp` | 1200×1800 |

- PNG encoding is deferred until after the toss.
- Edit tools: stickers (drag, pinch-rotate, 0°/±15° snap, ≤20), "Aa" text in verified fonts only, date stamp `12.11.2026` in orange LED style, filters (`booth.filter.natural` · `booth.filter.peach` · `booth.filter.milk` ("Kem sữa") · `booth.filter.film` · `booth.filter.bw` · `booth.filter.mist`), and undo/redo with 30 steps.

### 5.8 Accessibility

- Hero objects are `<button>` elements with Vietnamese `aria-label`s (`a11y.envelope`, `a11y.box`, `a11y.insertCoin`, `a11y.notebook`) and respond to Enter/Space (a single press, never a double tap).

### 6.4 Typography and text-handling rules

The §3.4 rules apply, plus:
- **NFC on every input**, at input time and again on the server.
- **Grapheme limits and animation** via `Intl.Segmenter('vi')`. Fallback regex `/\P{M}\p{M}*/gu` for WebViews older than Chrome 87.
- **No keydown filtering.** Telex and VNI compose through IME events (Gboard, Laban Key, Unikey). Validate on `input` / `compositionend`.
- **Locale-aware case and sorting:** `toLocaleUpperCase('vi')`, `Intl.Collator('vi')`.

- **Detect TCVN3/VNI mojibake** and warn; never silently convert.

### 6.5 Avoid list (legal, political, religious, funeral, kitsch)

- **National symbols.** No red field with a yellow five-pointed star, no hammer and sickle, no leaders, no state emblems. Red + gold is fine as lacquer or mai, never as a flag pattern.
- **Maps.** **Decided (§7.1): the memory map is an original painted city-plate map with no basemap, no national borders and no geocoding**, so no tile can carry a sovereignty error. If a real basemap is ever added, it must show Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa as part of Vietnam, label the sea "Biển Đông", never show the nine/ten-dash line, and pass a country-zoom QA test (§7.8).
- **Religion.** No Buddha figures, altars, incense, deities (Thần Tài, Ông Táo) or Christian sacred imagery. The lotus is fine as a flower only, never on flirty frames. No Vu Lan or "tháng cô hồn" themes. Thất Tịch stays on the legend.
- **Funeral cues** (critical for B):
  - Never a single centred black-and-white portrait with a black frame or ribbon (it reads as *di ảnh*).
  - No large white chrysanthemum arrangements and no white headbands.
  - Black-and-white 4-cut strips are fine with a light frame.
- **Money.** Never draw Vietnamese banknotes. A lì xì shows only the envelope.
- **No Hán tự decoration** (福, 囍). Use original quốc ngữ calligraphy ("An", "Thương", "Phúc").
- **Kitsch.** No rice-field + nón lá + buffalo postcards. One nón lá sticker at most. Áo dài only as an elegant silhouette, never sexualised.

### 7.3 The Director (choreography runtime)

```ts
// generated in motion.ts
type Step = { name: string; at: number; dur: number; target: string; action: ActionId; params?: Record<string, unknown>; ease?: EasingName; spring?: SpringName; tierMin?: Tier };
type Spec = { id: string; title: string; t0: string; assets: string[]; reduced: string; steps: Step[]; clock?: 'ticker' | 'gesture' | 'audio' };
interface Run { finished: Promise<void>; skip(): void; pause(): void; resume(): void; seek(ms: number): void }
interface Director { play(spec: Spec, refs: TargetMap, o: { timeScale?: number; reduced?: boolean; tier: Tier }): Run }
```

- **Specs are data** (`motion.ts → choreography`, keyed `C1a` … `C13`). Each step is named after its §4.3 table row (for example `C2.lidFlip3D`), which enables "jump to label" in previews and QA. `clock: 'gesture'` specs (C2a) are scrubbed by input progress, not by the ticker.
- **Three renderers on one clock:**
  - DOM steps go to Motion `animate()` sequences.
  - Canvas steps go to `fx.emit` (closed-form sprites evaluated at the clock time).
  - Text and state steps are 0 ms hard cuts (`setState` at the offset).
- **One rAF ticker is the sole clock.** Canvas fx read it directly. DOM sequences start on a ticker frame and are re-seeked (`animation.time = …`) only when drift exceeds 1 frame, or on skip, scrub or ×3 preview. This keeps WAAPI compositor offload in the normal case. The ticker sleeps when no run is active. The week-1 spike confirms the drift behaviour.
- **`run.finished` drives prompts** and machine events (`CHOREO_DONE`). Input is ignored until the next interactive state (G15).
- **`Director.play` awaits `preload(spec.assets)`** (`img.decode()` / `createImageBitmap`) before t0 (G6). Asset ids are symbolic (`atlas:bloom`, `img:envelope.open`, `page:letter`) and resolve through the theme manifest.
- **Circuit breaker:** ≥3 dropped frames within 50 ms → `skip()`, and the next spectacle degrades to the 300 ms crossfade (G18).
- **Reduced motion** swaps the spec for its reduced variant. **Preview** sets `timeScale: 3`.
- **Feature detection:** CSS `linear()` and the compositor path. On the low tier and on old WebViews, hot-path steps use a single `transform` string.
- **Curtain mask sweep, wash and fade** are drawn on the fx canvas (whole-layer `globalAlpha` + clip rect), so the low tier keeps them.
- **Arcs** (toss, hero flower throw) are quadratic Béziers evaluated on the ticker.
- **After the C1 bloom settles**, the emitter stops and the 12.5 vh/s drift becomes a CSS `translateY` on pre-rendered wall tiles.
- **Typewriter:** one rAF flips an `.on` class on spans up to `n = floor((now − t0)/ms)`, so it is time-anchored.

### 7.4 Pipelines

**Assets.** `assets-src/<theme>/<id>/*.png` (2× masters with alpha, plus `provenance.yaml`) → `tools/build-assets` (sharp) produces:
- WebP q80-85 (AVIF for plates ≥200 KB), at 1× and 2×.
- Atlases at 2048² (1024² on the low tier) with 2 px extrude.
- Print skins as exact-size PNG.
- **Pre-composited wall tiles** for the C1 drift and the C7 low-tier wall.
- One SFX sprite (Opus + AAC).
- `manifest.<hash>.json` (theme, mechanic, acts{critical, act2…}, bytes, tier variants).
- Output goes to `/assets/<hash>/…` with `Cache-Control: immutable`.

Loading:
- The critical act-1 set loads before landing.
- The next act's assets are fetched and decoded during reading holds.
- At most 2 atlases are decoded at once.
- **CI fails on any asset without a provenance entry.** Reference frames never enter the repo, not even as test fixtures.

**Booth.** §5.4. Memory peak per session ≤90 MB: 4 shots + compositor canvases + JPEG-encoded boomerang frames, with every `ImageBitmap.close()`d.

**Gift booth mechanic.** The sender's strips are **composited at publish time on the server** from the same layout definitions as B, so the recipient's phone only animates them.

**Publish.**
1. Validate `GiftContentV1`.
2. Freeze `gift_versions`.
3. Mint the per-channel slugs.
4. Render the OG image (closed hero + names, or `og.anonymousTitle` ("Ai đó gửi bạn một món quà") when `og_show_names=false`; **never photos or letter text**).
5. Composite the booth strips.
6. Bump `og_version`.

**Open.** `POST /api/gift/:slug/open`:
1. Resolve the slug. Revoked or expired → 410 soft page.
2. Unlock gate: no content before `unlock_at`.
3. Secret-question gate: scrypt of the normalised answer (§6.4) against `secret_answer_hash`, plus rate limits. On success, set a `gp_<slug>` httpOnly cookie for 7 days.
4. Return the content plus **signed media URLs** (6 h TTL) from a private bucket.

**Recipients never query Supabase directly.**

### 7.8 Testing

- **Token tests.**
  - `build.py` re-simulates every spring (`springs.py`) and fails if `peakFinal` drifts or the visible overshoot exceeds the spring's tier (0 / 1.5 / 4 / 6%). It compares `peakFinal`, never `overshootPctOfTravel`; for the stamp (mounted above final) the metric is the dip to 0.974.
  - In Vitest, sample the compiled `linear()` twins at 30 fps and assert the same peaks on the rendered property.
  - `build.py` also fails on: strip parts not summing to the canvas, spring steps whose `dur` ≠ `settleMs`, unknown actions or inline colours in steps, pure white / neutral grey / near-black theme colours, sub-minimum Vietnamese line heights or sizes, and over-budget font plans.
  - Assert that `tokens.css` and `motion.ts` are regenerated from the JSON (`build.py`, then `git diff --exit-code`) in CI.
- **String tests.** `build_vi.py` (NFC, parity, lowercase pill keys) and `check_framework_strings.py` (every quoted string in this document is a `vi.json` key with the same value).
- **Font tests.** `font_glyph_check.py` on every shipped WOFF2 (74/74; `--full` for fonts that render names in caps).
- **Headless scrub tests.** Seek each choreography to key offsets and assert property values against the reference frames. For example: C2 `lidFlip3D` at its midpoint (v4 f136-143); the C3 toss peak scale ≈1.054 at ~370 ms (v1 f137→f148); the C1 curtain at 105 vh by +1400.
- **Text tests.** Vietnamese torture strings in every font role, with no clipping in pills, stamps, the print slot or canvas strips. NFD paste into the typewriter.
- **Privacy test.** Zero network requests carry image bytes during a full booth session.
- **Device matrix (bought, not emulated):**
  - one Snapdragon 4xx and one MediaTek Helio phone with 3 GB RAM
  - an iPhone SE (2nd gen) class device
  - each in Chrome, the Zalo in-app browser, the Messenger in-app browser and Samsung Internet
  - an **old-WebView build** (without `linear()`) included
- **Webview matrix** (6 devices × Zalo, Messenger, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram; gUM, downloads, file share) **re-tested monthly as a release gate**, with results in `e2e/webview-matrix.md`.

## 8. Build roadmap

- The motion kit comes first because every product surface is built from it.
- The photobooth comes next because it needs no backend and validates the kit, the fx engine and the webview ladder on real phones.

## 9. Anti-patterns to avoid

**Seen in the references** (audit §3, ux.md §8):

| # | Anti-pattern | Evidence | Do instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Full-screen flashes faster than 3 per second (4 flashes 240 ms apart at .77-.85 + a .93 glow) | v1 #13-17 f54-82 | ≥1 s between full-screen flashes (2.4 s in the booth), peak ≤.85, .4 under reduced motion; the gift booth flashes the booth window only |
| A2 | Media UI desync: reels spin after pause | v5 C-e13, C-e15 f741-817 | Bind indicators to player events; no state source → no animation |
| A3 | Decode jank and pop-in: a 200 ms freeze, then late photos and random sticker pops | v3 M25-M26 f514-522 | `await decode()` before navigating; pop on purpose with a fixed delay and an 80-120 ms stagger |
| A4 | Native fullscreen breaks the page (scroll 100 → 41, inner scroll reset, FAB blink) | v5 f709-710 | In-app theatre overlay (FLIP 450 ms + rgba(10,6,8,.92)) |
| A5 | Navigation chrome live above modal scrims; tapping the CTA under the scrim snaps the page | v4 D5, D8-D9 | Hide or make inert the continue object and drawer while any modal or lightbox is open; only audio may stay above a reading scrim |
| A6 | Teardown glitches: a 1-frame tooltip ghost, a FAB blink, content cut while still visible | v1 #47 f471-472; v2b D01b f808 | Tear chrome down in one commit, under a cover or with a ≥100 ms fade |
| A7 | Jump scrolls inside drawers (+337 px, 50 → 385 px) | v4 D2 f403; v1 #43 f304 | Smooth 600-667 ms `inOutQuad` scroll, scroll-snap, sections |
| A8 | Prompt timers that ignore animation state, and unguarded taps | v5 A-e10 f152 vs f156 | Prompt on `run.finished`; debounce; queue at most one tap |
| A9 | Dead ends and silent affordances: no press state, no completion cue, no end control, an arrow cursor on a tappable wall, an unreadable subtitle | v3 f110-131, f605-669; v5 C-e09; v2a `#E8D8DD` on cream | Press feedback; a completion cue 600-1000 ms after typing; a replay/next action; `cursor:pointer`; AA contrast |
| A10 | TikTok-speed timing and capture artifacts copied as design (280 ms digits, 1-frame "brighten" frames, haze snaps, ghosted crossfades, duplicated frames) | v1 §10; v3 M15/M20; v2a C-M4 | Real-world cadence; shared elements pixel-stable or FLIPped; never reproduce stalls, hitches or watermarks |
| A11 | Interaction that depends on hover (chrome on first pointer move) | v5 A-e01 f17 | Timer-based chrome mount on touch (G17) |
| A12 | A "no thanks" branch with an unknown or punishing outcome | v3 f230-267 | A kind "Để sau" hold page that keeps the way forward; no dodging buttons |
| A13 | Fixed-height inner-scroll cards for media | v5 §7, B-11 | Bottom-sheet detents with a single scroll container |

**Implementation anti-patterns (ours to avoid):**
- Raw cubic-beziers, ms literals or hex colours in components. Shipping the overfit raw fits (for example v1 #15 `cubic-bezier(1,1.067,.755,.891)` labelled "linear") instead of the named family (audit 1.19).
- Overshoot on drawers, docks, modals, lids or pages. Any peak above 6%. Treating "% of travel" as "% of size".
- Tweening a status label. Typing text by code unit (detached tone marks). Typing a 400-grapheme letter at 8 ms/char (use the 25 ms floor, then per-word).
- `100vh`. Black letterboxing. Neutral-black scrims or shadows. Blur scrims or `backdrop-filter` on the low tier. Animated `box-shadow` or `filter`.
- Any font outside the 74/74 list for user text (Caveat, Fredoka, Archivo Black, Bebas Neue …). Letter-spaced or fake-italic script. Line heights under the Vietnamese minimums. Clip containers without top padding.
- Ambient motion everywhere on an interactive screen. Beat-locking ambient layers. Claiming beat sync with third-party embeds.
- Starting a cover before the destination is decoded. Encoding the PNG during the toss. Holding 60 full-resolution bitmaps for a boomerang.
- Inline alpha-video for the bloom in webviews (a playback lottery, and 1-2 MB). Rive or PixiJS on the recipient route.
- Trusting UA sniffing over the `getUserMedia` result. Calling `getUserMedia` before the explainer tap. Leaving the camera on while idle.
- Serving locked content to the client. Guessable slugs. OG images with photos or letter text. User-uploaded MP3s. Scraping Zing.
- `text-transform: capitalize` on Vietnamese. Keydown filtering in text inputs. Dates computed with a UTC+8 lunar library or by dividing timestamps by 86 400 000.
- Tracing or painting over reference frames. Reusing the reference English strings, names or layout one to one. Anything resembling the v3 licensed mascot (dogs, beagles, black-outline line art, top-edge peeking).
- **Reproducing the vendor's component set with a new coat of paint:** a heart FAB paired with a "there's more" pill, a rose continue icon with a "finish reading first" tooltip, and a plum drawer holding exactly voice + bouquet + song + map. Keep the patterns, change the surface: a painted drawer pull or theme glyph, a theme-specific continue object, freshly written copy (never translated), and our own drawer set (voice, mixtape, map, day count).
- Quoting UI copy in docs or code that is not a `vi.json` key. Step durations on a spring that differ from its settle time. Capture-pixel numbers pasted as CSS px.

### 10.4 Unresolved decisions (owner: you)
